## Main Result

Claude located Michael Copley Sr.'s recorded will in FamilySearch's **West Virginia Will Books, 1756-1971** collection.

The will is a high-confidence identity match. It was written in 1893 and probated in Lewis County on **4 March 1897**, six weeks after Michael's death on **19 January 1897**.

The will does **not** name Michael's parents. It does confirm the Lewis County family structure around his wife, son John, four unmarried or home-place daughters, and three cash-bequest children.

## Order Book v. 6 Page 496 Check

A later Claude for Chrome report located and read **County Court Order Book v. 6, page 496**, Film **825098** / DGS **8614118**, image **285 of 365**, ARK `https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C34Z-VSMP-H`.

The page is confirmed by the printed page number `496` on the left page. It contains two entries:

- a June 1900 notary-public qualification for **John H. Conrad**, attested by **E. A. Bennett**, Clerk;
- a c. June-July 1900 administration-bond entry for **Susan Woofter** as administratrix of **Marshall J. Woofter**, with **Charles Woofter** as surety.

No **Michael Copley**, **John Copley**, or Copley-variant entry appears on page 496. The C-section index on images 7-9 was also checked and did not show a Copley entry.

This creates a citation problem rather than a new family fact. The will certificate's `Order Book No. 6, page 496` citation may refer to a separate probate / will / administration order-book series, or the page number may have been copied or read incorrectly. Do not repeat this specific Board of Commissioners page-496 check unless verifying the negative.

## Abstract

Michael Copley identifies himself as being of Lewis County, West Virginia, and publishes his last will and testament. He leaves land to his son **John Copley**, including a fifty-five-acre tract and a larger one-hundred-twenty-seven-and-a-half-acre arrangement described by metes / boundary logic.

He directs that another land tract be divided among four daughters, **Annie**, **Bridget**, **Margaret**, and **Sarah**, after their mother's death. Those four daughters are also to inherit his personal property with his wife, then divide it after their mother's death, after paying funeral expenses and placing a decent stone on the parents' grave.

The four daughters' portion is charged with cash payments:

- **Catherne [Hannon?]**: $50.
- **Thomas [Copley]**: $50.
- **[Mary?] [Quinn?]**: $25.

Michael appoints his son **John Copley** as sole administrator / executor.

The will was written on **2 February 1893** and probated on **4 March 1897** before the Lewis County County Court. The probate certificate cites **Order Book No. 6, page 496** and is attested by **L. A. Bonnett**, Clerk.

## Parentage Relevance

The will does not corroborate or contradict the death-register parent names **John Copley** and **Sarah Copley**. It does not name Michael's parents and does not give Sarah's maiden surname as Dolan.

Its value is instead identity and family-structure confirmation:

- It confirms Michael Copley of Lewis County as the same man whose will was probated shortly after the January 1897 death.
- It confirms John Copley as son and executor.
- It confirms four daughters, Annie, Bridget, Margaret, and Sarah, as major heirs.
- It confirms Thomas, Catherine / Catherne, and likely Mary as cash-bequest children.
- It implies that Michael's wife, Ann / Anna Munday Copley, survived him or was living when the will was written in 1893.

The follow-up report restates the 1897 death-register parent names, but that does not add a new independent source beyond the already captured death-register evidence. The death register remains important but cautious evidence because the same row gives Lewis County rather than Ireland as birthplace and identifies Bridget Copley as wife / source.

## Copley Heirs Oil Clue

The follow-up also identified oil-industry references to a **Copley heirs' farm** / **Copley Heirs Well No. 1** at Sand Fork around 1900-1901. A later oil-title search verified the stronger formal designation **Michael Copley Heirs 128-Acre Lease** and corrected the useful contemporary No. 3 search term to `Copley heirs' No. 3`; the earlier `John Copley no. 3` reading should not be treated as verified.

This is not parentage evidence, but it strengthens the next search path. The published sources confirm the collective heirs-lease designation, while deed books, oil-and-gas leases, royalty assignments, partition deeds, or title-chain records may name the individual signers.
