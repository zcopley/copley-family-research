# 1883 Perrin Marion Partlow Biography

This note preserves the June 2026 web result locating the Partlow passage in the original county-history source. The source is **not** an 1888 Clark County history. It is William Henry Perrin's 1883 *History of Crawford and Clark Counties, Illinois*.

Raw report: [1883 Perrin Marion Partlow biography output](/static/sources/claude-partlow/1883-perrin-marion-partlow-biography-claude-output.txt)

## Citation

William Henry Perrin, ed., *History of Crawford and Clark Counties, Illinois* (Chicago: O. L. Baskin & Co., Historical Publishers, 1883), 138, biography of Marion Partlow, Johnson Township; Internet Archive identifier `historyofcrawfor00perr`; page image: https://archive.org/details/historyofcrawfor00perr/page/138/mode/2up?q=Partlow; OCR text: https://archive.org/stream/historyofcrawfor00perr/historyofcrawfor00perr_djvu.txt.

## Extract

The biography states that Marion Partlow was a farmer at Bell Air, Clark County, Illinois, and that the Partlow family came to Clark County in fall 1839. It identifies Marion's father as **John H. Partlow**, says John H. was born in 1811 "in one of the Middle States," and explicitly says John H. **"was a son of Jacob Partlow."**

The same passage says John H. came from Indiana to Clark County in 1839 with wife **Lydia Bennett**, entered 80 acres in Section 35 of Johnson Township, later went to Arkansas for his health, and died there. It says Lydia bore seventeen children, with six living when the sketch was written: Marion, Columbus N., and four sisters.

The Marion paragraph says Marion was born **27 Feb 1844** on the farm he then owned; married **Martha L. Bowles**, daughter of Wilson Bowles; and had three surviving children from that marriage after Martha's March 1881 death: **Nollie Franklin**, Henry A., and Orie D.

## Evidence Assessment

This source directly supports:

- **John H. Partlow -> Jacob Partlow** as a father-son claim.
- **Marion Partlow -> John H. Partlow and Lydia Bennett** as the local-history family frame.
- **Nollie Franklin Partlow -> Marion Partlow and Martha L. Bowles** as the next-generation family frame.

This source does **not** support:

- **Jacob Partlow -> Benjamin Partlow**.
- Any claim that the Perrin biography traces Jacob back through Benjamin or John Partlow II.

Benjamin Partlow is absent from the Partlow passage and from the book's relevant Partlow text. The earlier "1888 history" lead was a date/source-label error from a later social-media paraphrase or snippet trail. For this project, cite the source as **1883 Perrin, page 138**, and limit its proof value to **John H. -> Jacob** and Marion's immediate family details.

## Source Type

The Perrin sketch is a county-history / local-biographical source, probably based on information supplied by Marion Partlow or someone close to him around 1882-1883. It is valuable near-contemporary family testimony, but it is not a probate, deed, Bible, or vital record. Use it as strong derivative / testimonial evidence, not as a primary record.
