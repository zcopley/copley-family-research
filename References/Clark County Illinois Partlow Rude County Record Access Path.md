## Result

No original, reconstructed, or derivative **county** record has been found online for the Frank Partlow / Alice Rude marriage or license return. The located evidence remains the newspaper license notice in the 14 Jun 1900 *Clark County Herald*, which proves a license was issued under **8 Jun 1900** but does not prove the ceremony date, officiant, witnesses, or return.

## Survival Assessment

The post-1899 Clark County marriage record may survive, but this cannot be determined from online sources. The known constraints are:

- FamilySearch and the Illinois index do not supply the 1900 record.
- The FamilySearch Clark County marriage films stop before the target event.
- The 30 Dec 1902 courthouse fire caused some record loss or damage, but the online IRAD language does not establish total loss.
- Clark County's genealogy-certificate pathway implies at least some old marriage records remain with the Clerk.
- The Marshall Public Library may hold the Clark County Genealogical Society's `Extract of marriages from Clark County, 1819-1900`, which is exactly the compiled source most likely to include a 1900 extract if the compilers had access to the record.
- FamilySearch Library holds confirmed physical and microfilm copies of the extract, but there is no online camera access. A FamilySearch Library in-person lookup is a fallback if Marshall Public Library cannot check the volume.

## Evidence Boundary

The current evidence supports:

- A Clark County marriage license was issued under **8 Jun 1900** to **Frank Partlow**, age 25, of Moonshine, and **Alice Rude**, age 19.
- The reported family marriage date of about **10 Jun 1900** is plausible and strongly supported by the license notice.

The current evidence does **not** yet prove:

- exact ceremony date;
- officiant;
- witnesses;
- license number or register page;
- parental names;
- whether the county return survived;
- whether Frank Partlow is explicitly the same person as Frank N. / Nollie Franklin Partlow in later records.
