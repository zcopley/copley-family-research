# Clark County Illinois 1900 Partlow Rude Census Check

## Purpose

Check whether **Frank / Nollie Franklin Partlow** and **Alice / Mary Alice Rude** appear together in the 1900 U.S. federal census around the reported **10 Jun 1900** Clark County, Illinois marriage.

## Result

They do **not** appear together as a married couple. Both were found in **Johnson Township, Clark County, Illinois, ED 8**, and both were recorded as **single**:

- **Frank N Partlow**, age 25, born Apr 1875 in Illinois, single, nephew in or adjacent to the Columbus Partlow household, sheet 7A.
- **Mary A Rude**, age 20, born Oct 1880 in Illinois, single, daughter in the John A. Rude household, sheet 7B.

This is consistent with a reported marriage on **10 Jun 1900**, because the census reference date was **1 Jun 1900**. The census does not prove the marriage, but it supports the timing and neighborhood context: both proposed spouses were single, age-appropriate, and living in the same township / enumeration district immediately before the reported marriage.

## Evidence Value

This is **circumstantial support**, not proof of marriage or identity. It shows that Frank N. Partlow and Mary A. Rude were both in Johnson Township, Clark County, Illinois, at the 1 Jun 1900 census reference date, both still single, and both close in the enumerator's sequence. Because a 10 Jun 1900 marriage would fall after the census reference date, the single status does not contradict the family-source marriage date.

The direct proof targets remain:

1. Clark County Clerk / Recorder search for any surviving or reconstructed marriage record, license, return, application, affidavit, or index card corresponding to the newspaper-reported **8 Jun 1900** license.
2. Later death, obituary, cemetery, and household records tying **Frank N. / Nollie Franklin Partlow** to **Alice / Mary Alice Rude Partlow**.
