# Clark County Herald 1900 Partlow Rude Marriage License Notice

Raw Claude for Chrome source note: [Partlow-Rude 1900 Clark County Herald marriage-license output](/static/sources/claude-partlow/partlow-rude-1900-clark-county-herald-marriage-license-claude-output.txt).

## Purpose

Preserve the newspaper notice found for the reported **10 Jun 1900** marriage of **Frank / Nollie Franklin Partlow** and **Alice / Mary Alice Rude** in Clark County, Illinois.

## Result

A marriage-license notice was found in the **Clark County Herald** of Marshall, Illinois, dated **Thursday, 14 Jun 1900**. In the `Marriage Licenses` column, under the date heading **June 8**, the paper lists:

```text
Frank Partlow, 25, Moonshine.
Alice Rude, 19.
```

This confirms that a Clark County marriage license was issued to **Frank Partlow** and **Alice Rude** on **8 Jun 1900**. It strongly supports the family-source marriage date of about **10 Jun 1900**, but it is a license notice rather than a ceremony notice. It does not give the exact ceremony date, officiant, witnesses, parents, or original license number.
