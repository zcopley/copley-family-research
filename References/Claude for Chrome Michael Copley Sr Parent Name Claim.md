# Claude for Chrome Michael Copley Sr Parent Name Claim

This note preserves Zach's June 2026 Claude for Chrome check of the reported parent-name clue for [[People/Michael Copley Sr|Michael Copley Sr.]].

[Download raw Claude report](../static/sources/claude-michael-parents/michael-copley-sr-parent-name-claim-claude-output.txt)

[Download WV Culture death-register image](../static/sources/claude-michael-parents/michael-copley-death-register-4130795-00393.jpg)

## Main Result

The official WV Culture death-record detail page and original death-register image support **John Copley** and **Sarah Copley** as the parent names recorded for Michael Copley Sr. in the 1897 Lewis County death register.

They do **not** support **Sarah Dolan** as a maiden name. No Dolan maiden name appears in the WV Culture index or on the original register row.

The same row contains serious reliability warnings: it records **Bridget Copley** as Michael's consort / wife and as the information source, even though the broader family evidence identifies Michael's wife as [[People/Ann Copley|Ann / Anna Munday Copley]] and Bridget as a daughter; WV Culture also indexes Michael's birthplace as Lewis County rather than Ireland. That makes the parent names important evidence, but not final proof.

## Interpretation

This is now the strongest located record for Michael's parent names, but it is not enough to prove the full John Copley + Sarah Dolan claim.

Evidence supported by the death register:

- The 1897 Lewis County death register names Michael's parents as John and Sarah Copley.
- The parent names are on an official county death-register source, not merely in a user-submitted tree.
- The exact-name WV Culture search for `Michael Copley` can miss the record because the index spells the given name `Micheal`.

Evidence not supported:

- The death register does not name Sarah's maiden surname.
- It does not say Sarah was a Dolan.
- It does not prove that the named John and Sarah were from Kilgefin or that they were the same couple in any family-tree reconstruction.

Reliability caution:

The death register is a primary source for Michael's death, but the parent names are secondary information. The row's `Bridget Copley` wife / informant error and Lewis County birthplace indexing show that whoever supplied, recorded, or indexed the family-context data was confused about important identity details. The parent names should therefore be treated as a high-value lead that needs corroboration from probate, Bible, church, Irish parish, or estate records.

## Next Research Target

The best next step is no longer "find the death-index clue"; that clue has been found and tied to the original WV Culture image.

Next targets:

- Request or locate Michael Copley Sr.'s Lewis County will / probate / estate packet after his 19 January 1897 death.
- Search oil-title, heirship, chancery, and probate files for Michael Copley heirs, especially any family affidavit, Bible extract, or relationship schedule.
- Continue treating Sarah Dolan as unproven until an original or near-original record names Sarah with the Dolan surname.
- If possible, obtain a clearer crop or courthouse copy of Death Register volume 1, page 231, to preserve the Copley row and the Bridget relationship error cleanly.
