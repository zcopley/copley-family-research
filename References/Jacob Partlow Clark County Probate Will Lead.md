# Jacob Partlow Clark County Probate Will Lead

This note preserves the June 2026 report locating a derivative / snippet trail for the **last will and testament of Jacob Partlow**, Clark County, Illinois. The original will image has **not** yet been obtained, so this is a located probate lead rather than final proof.

Raw report: [Jacob Partlow probate/will lead output](/static/sources/claude-partlow/jacob-partlow-clark-county-probate-will-lead-claude-output.txt)

## Located Will Lead

Reported probate details:

- **Testator:** Jacob Partlow / Jacob Newton Partlow.
- **Will date:** 3 Sep 1867.
- **Filed / probated:** 26 May 1868, Clark County Clerk's Office, Marshall, Illinois.
- **Witnesses:** Jacob Geffs and Royer S. Davis.
- **Executor named:** William W. Willison.
- **Original repository:** Clark County courthouse / probate records, Marshall, Illinois.

The will was reported through two derivative routes:

1. Find a Grave memorial `135126498`, Jacob Newton "Nute" Partlow.
2. Harry C. Partlow, *Clark County Illinois Partlows*, formerly available through a FamilySearch patron-uploaded `dist.txt` file and now recoverable only through search snippets.

## Reconstructed Will Abstract

The recovered snippets report that Jacob's will gave wife **Polly (Mary)** "all for life" and then stated that he had already given all heirs **$40.00 apiece**. The residue apparently went to **William**, described in the snippets as receiving all remaining personal and real estate, except that **Polly Ritenhouse / Ridenhouse** was to receive a cow and a bed.

Working interpretation:

- **Polly (Mary)** is probably Mary / Polly Abrams Partlow, Jacob's wife in the 1860 household and the woman tied to the 1815 Indiana marriage record.
- **William** is probably William Franklin Partlow, in Jacob's 1860 household and named by derivative memorials as Jacob's son.
- **Polly Ritenhouse / Ridenhouse** is likely the young girl in the 1860 Jacob household, but her relationship to Jacob remains undetermined in the snippet trail.
- The phrase about all heirs receiving $40 each is important because it suggests unnamed heirs may have been advanced or settled before the 1867 will.

## What This Does and Does Not Prove

This lead supports:

- A Clark County probate / will record exists for Jacob Partlow.
- Wife Polly / Mary was living when the will was drafted.
- William was a favored residual beneficiary.
- Jacob recognized a broader heir group, but the snippet does not name them.

This lead does **not** yet prove:

- John H. Partlow was one of Jacob's heirs.
- Mary Halleck was Jacob's first wife.
- Jacob's parents or birthplace.
- Jacob's relationship to Benjamin Partlow.

## John H. Partlow

The reconstructed will text does **not** name John H. Partlow. The companion Harry C. Partlow genealogy snippet reportedly names **John Halleck Partlow** as a child of Jacob Partlow and Mary Halleck, and describes John as a nephew of Isaac Partlow. That supports the existing derivative claim **John H. -> Jacob**, but it remains a compiled-source claim until the original supporting records are found.

The will's "all my heirs $40.00 apiece" language may explain why adult or earlier-settled children were not named in the residuary clause, but that inference needs the original will text and probate packet.

## Mary Halleck and Mary / Polly Abrams

The new report proposes a two-marriage structure:

1. Jacob Partlow and **Mary Halleck**, about 1810, with children John Halleck Partlow and James Halleck Partlow III, based on compiled Harry C. Partlow / memorial snippets.
2. Jacob Partlow and **Mary / Polly Abrams**, 13 Sep 1815 in Franklin County, Indiana, based on the Indiana marriage index / FHL film `000549348`.

For the project, this resolves the earlier apparent conflict only as a **working hypothesis**. The Polly Abrams marriage is supported by an indexed record; the Mary Halleck marriage still needs an original or near-original record.
