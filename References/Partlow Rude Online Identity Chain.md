## Source Cautions

- FamilySearch Family Tree and Find a Grave are derivative platforms. They are useful here because they point to and cluster original / near-original records, not because their user-created conclusions are independently authoritative.
- The 14 Jun 1900 newspaper notice proves license issuance on 8 Jun 1900. It does **not** prove the ceremony return, officiant, witnesses, or exact ceremony date.
- The Marion Find a Grave memorial reportedly lists Partlow Cemetery / Oak Point, Clark County, Illinois. The project already has family confirmation that Marion was buried at Sunset View Cemetery, El Cerrito, California, with Michael Joseph Copley. Treat the Find a Grave burial location as conflicting derivative data until checked against cemetery records.
- Alice / Mary Alice's standalone open Find a Grave memorial page and obituary were not found online in this pass.
