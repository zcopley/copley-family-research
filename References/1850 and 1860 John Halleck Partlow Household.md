# 1850 and 1860 John Halleck Partlow Household

This note records the original-image results for the 1850 and 1860 Johnson Township, Clark County, Illinois census households of **John H. / John Halleck Partlow** and **Lydia Bennett Partlow**.

Raw source report: [partlow-1850-1860-john-halleck-household-original-images-claude-output.txt](/static/sources/claude-partlow/partlow-1850-1860-john-halleck-household-original-images-claude-output.txt)

## Result

**Found.** The original census images support **Marion McDonald / Marion M. Partlow** as a child in the household of **John H. / John Partlow** and **Lydia / Sydia Partlow**.

This upgrades the **Marion McDonald Partlow -> John Halleck Partlow / Lydia Bennett** link from derivative citation to original-image census evidence.

## Discrepancies and Neighbor Notes

- **1860 page number:** New England Ball Project cited p. 65, but the original image and FamilySearch record panel show p. **135**. Treat the NEBP p. 65 as a citation discrepancy until another pagination system is identified.
- **1850 index:** FamilySearch indexed the surname as `Partton`; the original image supports `Partlow`.
- **1850 Marion name:** `Momon` / `Momore` is a difficult reading. The identification as Marion is based on household continuity and the clear 1860 `Marion M` entry.
- **1850 nearby Partlow:** Isaac Partlow appears seven pages earlier, image 302 / p. 543, as a likely relation.
- **1860 nearby Partlow:** Benjamin Partlow and Sarah An Partlow appear on the same page in a separate household immediately after John Partlow's household.
