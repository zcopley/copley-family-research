# Partlow Upstream Revolutionary Line Web Search

This note preserves the June 2026 web-only search pass for the upstream Partlow line beyond [[References/1850 and 1860 John Halleck Partlow Household|John H. / John Halleck Partlow]]. The task was to find online evidence for either **John H. Partlow -> Jacob Partlow** or **Jacob Partlow -> Benjamin Partlow**, without repeating the already completed 1880, 1850, and 1860 census-image work.

Raw report: [Partlow upstream Revolutionary-line web search output](/static/sources/claude-partlow/partlow-upstream-revolutionary-line-web-search-claude-output.txt)

## Result

**Later correction:** A subsequent primary-record pass found [[References/Rappahannock Partlow Chancery Case 1842-002]], whose complete 69-image file names Benjamin Partlow's eight child / heir lines and omits **Jacob / Jacob Newton / Nute Partlow** throughout. Therefore the derivative **Benjamin -> Jacob** claim summarized below is contradicted, not supported. This note remains useful as a record of the derivative-source trail that led to the chancery target.

The search substantially strengthens the upstream line with concordant online derivative sources, but it does not yet produce a primary online document that directly names **Jacob Newton Partlow** as a child or heir of **Benjamin Partlow**.

## Online Sources Found

### Partlow Book

Thomas E. Partlow's *The Partlow Family and Connections* was confirmed as a real two-part compiled genealogy:

- FamilySearch catalog ID `5481`: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/5481
- Two parts, 151 pages plus 193 pages, published 1973-1974.
- Holdings were also reported at the North Carolina State Archives and FamilySearch Library.

No open-access online scan was found through Google Books, HathiTrust, Internet Archive, FamilySearch catalog browsing, or RootsWeb library listings. This source remains useful but was not readable online in this pass.

### DAR And Pension Leads

The search found derivative confirmation that Benjamin Partlow is an accepted DAR patriot:

- Benjamin Partlow, DAR ancestor `A088405`: https://services.dar.org/Public/DAR_Research/search_adb/?action=full&p_id=A088405
- John Partlow, DAR ancestor `A088406`: https://services.dar.org/Public/DAR_Research/search_adb/?action=full&p_id=A088406
- WikiTree profile for Benjamin Partlow: https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Partlow-163
- WikiTree profile for John Partlow: https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Partlow-164

The DAR page itself was not directly accessible in the browser session, but WikiTree identifies Benjamin as DAR Patriot Ancestor `A088405` and John Partlow as `A088406`.

Benjamin's Revolutionary War pension file was identified as **S5871**. The RevWarApps PDF URL is:

- https://www.revwarapps.org/s5871.pdf

The PDF was blocked by a bot challenge, but the report found derivative transcript text through WikiTree and related snippets. The transcript describes Benjamin Partlow of Culpeper County, Virginia, applying for pension on 18 July 1832, serving as a private in Virginia militia, receiving a $20 annual allowance, and being listed as age 70.

### John Partlow II Will

WikiTree's John Partlow profile provides a transcript citing **Spotsylvania County, Virginia Will Book E, 1772-1798, Reel 28, pages 975-976**:

- https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Partlow-164

The relevant relationship language gives **Benjamin Partlow** as John's son and places Benjamin's inherited land in **Culpeper County**. The raw report quotes the clause as giving Benjamin 250 acres in Culpeper County.

This strongly supports **John Partlow II -> Benjamin Partlow**, and the Culpeper land reference coheres with Benjamin's later Culpeper pension identity. The next proof step is to capture the original will-book image or a reliable archive scan, not another derivative abstract.

### Jacob Newton Partlow

The search found multiple concordant derivative sources for Jacob Newton / Nute Partlow:

- FamilySearch Ancestors page for Mary Abrams, `LBFC-WWK`: https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LBFC-WWK/mary-abrams-1791-1882
- Find a Grave memorial for Benjamin Partlow, memorial `139145744`: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/139145744/benjamin-partlow
- Geni page for Lucy Menefee / Partlow: https://www.geni.com/people/Lucy-Menefee/6000000016000855001
- FamilySearch-hosted compiled text surfaced as `dist.txt`: https://www.familysearch.org/das-mem/patron/dist.txt
- Ancestry international snippet naming John Halleck Partlow's parents as Jacob Newton Partlow and Mary Halleck.

The strongest online derivative claim for **Benjamin -> Jacob** is the Find a Grave memorial for Benjamin Partlow listing **Jacob Newton "Nute" Partlow (1790-1868)** as a child. Geni's Lucy Menefee page also lists Jacob Newton Partlow among Benjamin's children. These are not primary records.

The strongest online derivative claim for **Jacob -> John H.** is the FamilySearch-hosted / Harry C. Partlow compiled genealogy snippet naming **John Halleck Partlow** as a child of Jacob Newton Partlow and Mary Halleck, plus the later-located 1883 Perrin county-history passage discussed below.

### 1883 Perrin County History

A later follow-up located the actual source behind the old "1888 history" snippet: William Henry Perrin, ed., *History of Crawford and Clark Counties, Illinois* (Chicago: O. L. Baskin & Co., 1883), p. 138. See [[1883 Perrin Marion Partlow Biography]].

- John H. Partlow was a son of Jacob Partlow.
- Marion Partlow is linked to this same chain.

The located Perrin passage does **not** say the descent from Jacob runs through Benjamin to John Partlow II. That Benjamin extension came from other derivative / compiled sources and is contradicted by the complete Rappahannock chancery heir file.

## Interpretation

At the time of this broad web pass, the Partlow status appeared to move from "upstream relationship not found online" to "upstream relationship strongly supported online by concordant derivative sources." Later primary-record work changed that interpretation: [[References/Rappahannock Partlow Chancery Case 1842-002]] names Benjamin Partlow's eight child / heir lines and omits Jacob / Jacob Newton / Nute from the complete 69-image file. Use the derivative sources below as leads, not as proof of the **Jacob Newton Partlow -> Benjamin Partlow** link.

The most important next online targets are:

1. **Culpeper County Chancery Papers, case #53, 1840-1841**, cited by WikiTree as the best source for Benjamin's children. Search the Library of Virginia Chancery Records Index for Partlow in Culpeper County.
2. **Culpeper County Deed Book FF, pages 394-395**, especially the reported 13 April 1813 gift deed from Benjamin and Clary to son Elisha. Search nearby deed indexes and pages for a comparable deed or distribution involving Jacob.
3. **Rappahannock County Will Book A, page 223**, Benjamin Partlow's estate inventory, February 1838. This may identify heirs, estate administrator, or sale context.
4. **Original Spotsylvania County Will Book E / p. 975-976 image** for John Partlow II's will, to replace online transcripts with an original or archive image.
5. This broad-pass target is now complete and corrected by [[1883 Perrin Marion Partlow Biography]]. The live target is no longer the county-history page; it is Jacob Partlow's original Clark County will / probate packet and original support for the Mary Halleck / Isaac Partlow claims.

## Caution

Do not state the full line as DAR-ready primary proof. The downstream chain through John H. is original-image supported, but the **Jacob -> Benjamin** relationship is contradicted by the complete Rappahannock chancery file unless a separate primary record explains an exceptional exclusion.
