## Main Result

The original WV Culture image is readable. It confirms that Lewis County Clerk **Jno / John Monroe** issued a marriage license for **Luke Murray** and **Anne Loghan** on **30 December 1854**.

The register entry does **not** name an officiant, witnesses, bondsman, surety, parents, guardian, or consent giver. It is a license-issuance entry rather than a solemnization return or bond packet.

## Transcription

Best reading of the target entry:

> Virginia / Lewis County Ct. [Clk.] / These are to license and permit you to join in the Holy Estate of Matrimony Luke Murray to Anne Loghan And for so doing this shall be your authority. Given when my hand this 30th day of Dec 1854 and 79th Year of the Commonwealth. Jno Monroe Clk

## Interpretation

The direct image confirms **Loghan** as the bride surname. The separate FamilySearch / WV Culture index form `Log?an` is an indexer's uncertainty, not a reason to prefer Logan over Loghan.

This record strengthens the 1854 same-county Loghan context but does not connect Luke / Anne to **John Loghan**, **Owen Loghan**, **James Murray**, **Patrick Murry**, **Donahoe / Donahue**, **C. Kester**, or the **John Ree** district. None of those associates are named in the register entry.

The result does not change the post-1850 first-tax-appearance model or the older Luke / Margaret problem. It confirms the younger Luke / Anne marriage date and bride surname, but it does not identify Luke's parents, Anne's parents, a bondsman, or a witness network.
