# Rappahannock Partlow Chancery Case 1842-002

This note preserves the June 2026 primary-record search result for the attempted proof that **Jacob Newton Partlow** was a child or heir of **Benjamin Partlow**, Revolutionary War pensioner. The critical finding is negative and corrective: the complete 69-image chancery file names Benjamin Partlow's heirs and **does not include Jacob / Jacob Newton / Nute Partlow**.

## Heirs Named In The Bill

The bill of complaint states that Benjamin Partlow died intestate in Rappahannock County on **23 Dec 1837** and was seized of four tracts of mountain land totaling about **804 acres**.

The bill names these living children and heirs:

- **John L. Partlow**
- **Elisha Partlow**
- **Sarah R. Morris**, relict of Edmund Morris
- **Lucy Menefee**, wife of John M. Menefee
- **Martha Behem**, wife of John Behem
- **Clarissa Cornwell**, wife of Isham Cornwell

It also names representatives of deceased daughters:

- Children of **Catherine Menefee**, deceased daughter of Benjamin Partlow, including Janet Menefee, Lucy Bolen / Bole, John Menefee, Isham Menefee, and other minor Menefee children noted in the report.
- **Thomas P. Bowran**, son of **Mary Bowran**, deceased daughter of Benjamin Partlow.

## Jacob Result

**Jacob Partlow / Jacob Newton Partlow / Nute Partlow does not appear anywhere in the 69-image file.**

This is the strongest online primary evidence located so far for Benjamin Partlow's children and heirs. It does not prove Jacob as Benjamin's child. It contradicts the derivative tree claim that Jacob Newton Partlow was Benjamin's son or heir.

The only `Jacob` in the file is **Jacob Menefee**, Benjamin's grandson through daughter **Lucy Partlow Menefee**. The file also mentions **Henry B. Partlow** and **Benjamin L. Partlow** as purchasers at the commissioner's land sale, not as defendants, heirs, or distributees.

## Interpretation

The chancery file names Benjamin Partlow's complete intestate heir class after his death. If Jacob Newton Partlow was alive until 1868, as derivative Clark County sources say, he should normally appear as a living child or heir if he were Benjamin's son. His complete absence is therefore decisive for this project unless another primary record explains an exceptional exclusion.

Current cautious conclusion:

- **Benjamin Partlow's Revolutionary service remains supported.**
- **John Partlow II -> Benjamin Partlow remains separately supported by will transcript / abstract leads.**
- **Jacob Newton Partlow -> John H. / John Halleck Partlow remains supported by derivative Clark County / compiled sources.**
- **Benjamin Partlow -> Jacob Newton Partlow is contradicted by the complete online chancery file and should not be stated as fact.**

Possible explanations to test:

1. Jacob Newton Partlow may belong to a different Benjamin Partlow family.
2. Jacob may be related through another child or collateral Partlow line rather than as Benjamin's son.
3. A deed, adoption, guardianship, or other non-heir relationship may explain the derivative chain, but no such proof has been found yet.
4. The derivative sources may have conflated Benjamin Partlow with another Partlow relative or skipped a generation.
