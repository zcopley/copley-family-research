# Rappahannock Partlow Chancery Case 1842-002

This note preserves the June 2026 primary-record search result for the attempted proof that **Jacob Newton Partlow** was a child or heir of **Benjamin Partlow**, Revolutionary War pensioner. The critical finding is negative and corrective: the best online primary record located so far names Benjamin Partlow's heirs and **does not include Jacob / Jacob Newton / Nute Partlow**.

## Heirs Named In The Bill

The bill of complaint states that Benjamin Partlow died intestate in Rappahannock County on **23 Dec 1837** and was seized of four tracts of mountain land totaling about **804 acres**.

The bill names these living children and heirs:

- **John L. Partlow**
- **Elisha Partlow**
- **Sarah R. Morris**, relict of Edmund Morris
- **Lucy Menefee**, wife of John M. Menefee
- **Martha Behem**, wife of John Behem
- **Clarissa Cornwell**, wife of Isham Cornwell

It also names representatives of deceased daughters:

- Children of **Catherine Menefee**, deceased daughter of Benjamin Partlow, including Janet Menefee, Lucy Bolen / Bole, John Menefee, Isham Menefee, and other minor Menefee children noted in the report.
- **Thomas P. Bowran**, son of **Mary Bowran**, deceased daughter of Benjamin Partlow.

## Jacob Result

**Jacob Partlow / Jacob Newton Partlow / Nute Partlow does not appear in the bill of complaint's heir list, advancement list, plaintiff / defendant list, or first four images reviewed.**

This is the strongest online primary evidence located so far for Benjamin Partlow's children and heirs. It does not prove Jacob as Benjamin's child. It substantially weakens, and may contradict, the derivative tree claim that Jacob Newton Partlow was Benjamin's son.

## Interpretation

The chancery bill appears to name Benjamin Partlow's complete intestate heir class after his death. If Jacob Newton Partlow was alive until 1868, as derivative Clark County sources say, he should normally appear as a living child or heir if he were Benjamin's son. His omission is therefore significant.

Current cautious conclusion:

- **Benjamin Partlow's Revolutionary service remains supported.**
- **John Partlow II -> Benjamin Partlow remains separately supported by will transcript / abstract leads.**
- **Jacob Newton Partlow -> John H. / John Halleck Partlow remains supported by derivative Clark County / compiled sources.**
- **Benjamin Partlow -> Jacob Newton Partlow is not supported by the best online primary record found so far and should not be stated as fact.**
