This page is the evidence backbone for the vault. It does not replace the detailed person, topic, and reference pages. Instead, it answers the control question for the whole project:

**What do we think we know, how strong is the evidence, and where is the proof trail?**

## What Is Mostly Proven Right Now

- Michael Copley Sr. and Patrick Copley jointly purchased land in Lewis County in 1843.
- A Catholic Copely family remained in the Fairymount / Kilgefin area after Michael's emigration.
- The 1837-1838 migration record trail through the *Kutusoff* and *Powhatan* is central to the family's American arrival story.
- Copley No. 1 was a real 1900 oil strike on family land.
- Captain John Copley is a documented 17th-century historical figure; the uncertainty is the later generational bridge, not his existence.

## What Is Still Open or Contested

- Michael Copley Sr.'s Irish parents remain unproven.
- Ann Copley's exact maiden name still lacks a direct record; `Munday` remains the recorded family form, while the `Murray` hypothesis is now downgraded to an unproven / probably mistaken lead unless direct evidence appears.
- The Murray Settlement framework is currently useful, but still a working explanatory model rather than a finished demonstration.
- The direct generation-by-generation bridge from Captain John Copley to the Roscommon Copleys remains unresolved.
- The oil-to-education / mobility connection is plausible, but the financial proof trail is still incomplete.
- The Partlow line is supported from Marion Elizabeth back to John H. / John Halleck Partlow by web-accessible original or near-original records, and John H. -> Jacob now has strong 1883 local-history plus compiled-source support. Jacob's Clark County will is located by derivative snippets, but the original will / probate packet is still needed. The proposed Revolutionary-line extension through Benjamin Partlow is contradicted: the complete Rappahannock Chancery Case `1842-002` file names Benjamin Partlow's eight child / heir lines and omits Jacob / Jacob Newton / Nute Partlow from all 69 images.

## Evidence Status Key

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Supported by a named primary source, image, record extract, or clearly identified contemporary source. |
| **Strong working conclusion** | Multiple independent clues point the same way, but a direct identity record is still missing. |
| **Plausible** | Reasonable synthesis or family-memory interpretation, but not yet proven. |
| **Oral / family tradition** | Preserved in family memory or family-authored notes; needs corroboration if used as fact. |
| **Unresolved** | Important claim or relationship remains open. |
| **Contradicted / rejected** | Earlier claim is contradicted by later evidence and should not be repeated as fact. |
