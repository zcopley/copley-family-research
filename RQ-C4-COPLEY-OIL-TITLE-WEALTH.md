# RQ-C4 Copley Oil Title and Wealth Dashboard

## Question

What exactly was the Copley oil-title chain around the 1900 strike, and can records prove how oil income affected family wealth, education, and mobility?

## Current Answer

The strike and property designation are verified in period sources as **Michael Copley Heirs well No. 1** and **Michael Copley Heirs 128-Acre Lease**. The exact Sand Fork lease text, royalty terms, lessors, and later distribution remain unresolved.
