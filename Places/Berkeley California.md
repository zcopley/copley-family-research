# Berkeley, California

## Geographic Information

- **Current administration:** Berkeley, Alameda County, California.
- **Family-region context:** Inner East Bay, adjacent to [[Places/Albany California|Albany]] and near El Cerrito and Richmond Annex.

## Copley Family Connection

Berkeley became the main California domestic setting for the [[People/Michael Joseph Copley|Michael Joseph Copley]] and [[People/Marion Elizabeth Partlow|Marion Elizabeth Partlow Copley]] household after Michael became Director of the USDA Western Regional Research Laboratory in nearby [[Places/Albany California|Albany]] in 1947.

The most important Berkeley family site is [[Places/862 Arlington Avenue Berkeley|862 Arlington Avenue]], the large house Michael bought in 1947 after its earlier ownership by Minnie Clough, an insurance agent, and Monroe Deutsch. The house became a childhood and family-gathering place for [[People/Stephen Michael Copley|Stephen Michael Copley]], [[People/Thomas Partlow Copley|Thomas Partlow Copley]], and later descendants.

Berkeley is also tied to:

- [[People/Stephen Michael Copley|Stephen Michael Copley]]'s Oxford Elementary, Berkeley High, and UC Berkeley years.
- [[People/Thomas Partlow Copley|Thomas Partlow Copley]]'s childhood, Berkeley High years, UC Berkeley education, and early relationship with [[People/Barbara Dee LaForce|Barbara Dee LaForce]].
- [[People/Erin E. Copley Bird|Erin Copley Bird]] and Bryan Bird's later stewardship of the Arlington Avenue family home.
