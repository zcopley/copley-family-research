# RQ-C1 Michael Copley Sr Parents Dashboard

## Question

Were [[People/Michael Copley Sr|Michael Copley Sr.]]'s parents John Copley and Sarah Copley, and was Sarah's maiden surname Dolan?

## Current Answer

The 1897 Lewis County death register records Michael's parents as **John Copley** and **Sarah Copley**, but the same record has reliability problems and does not give Sarah's maiden surname. **Sarah Dolan remains unproven.**

The operational control sheet is [[References/Michael Copley Sr Parentage Evidence Audit and Request Packet]].
