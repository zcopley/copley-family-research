# RQ-C6 Bredon and Captain John Copley Dashboard

## Question

Can the Copley line be documented from the Bredon / Woolbedding / Captain John Copley material down to the 19th-century Roscommon Copelys, including Michael Copley Sr.?

## Current Answer

Bredon / Captain John remains a preferred origin hypothesis, not a proven genealogy. The missing bridge is the Irish generation chain from the mid-17th century to Michael Copley Sr.'s 1813 birth era.

## Evidence Status

| Evidence | Status | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bredon / Woolbedding pedigree material | Contextual / plausible | Supports deep-origin hypothesis, not modern proof. |
| Captain John Copley research | Plausible lead | Needs generation-by-generation bridge. |
| Roscommon / Kilgefin Copely records | Partial | Supports Irish geography but not the full descent. |
| Michael Copley Sr. parentage evidence | Unresolved | Cannot connect to Bredon until John / Sarah and their origins are better proved. |

## Next Records

1. ORT-021: Irish bridge records from 1657-1813 linking Woolbedding / Bredon lines to Roscommon Copelys.
2. Search Irish land, probate, church, estate, and recusancy records for Copely / Copley variants.
3. Avoid extending the public tree through Captain John unless the missing generations are documented.

## Guardrail

Do not present Bredon descent as proven generation-by-generation. Keep it as a research hypothesis until the Irish bridge is documented.
