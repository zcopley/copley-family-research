# RQ-M1 Lewis County FAN Sweep

This page is the findings log for census and neighbor-network research around the Murray Settlement hypothesis in Lewis County, Virginia / West Virginia.

**Current status:** The bounded Claude for Chrome FAN-sweep passes for **1850**, **1860**, and **1870** are now complete, and the key 1860 / 1870 cluster pages now have a direct image-capture pass, a Murry continuity pass, a vital-record verification pass, an original-image transcription pass, two older-Luke / Margaret indexed-search passes, and a Lewis County personal-property-tax check. The 1850 pass found the **Micheal / Patrick Coopley** household in **District 30**, but no broader Irish surname cluster. The 1860 image capture confirms a dense Ireland-born cluster centered on **Dolan / Murry / Tierney** rural farming households, plus a separate **Weston** boarding-house / labor cluster around **Loftus / Barret / Mc Mahan** and other Ireland-born workers. The Murry continuity search shows **Luke Murry** moved from Lewis County to Providence, Rhode Island between late 1866 and March 1867, while the separate adult **Patrick Murry** stayed in Lewis County, appeared in Sheridan / Lewis in 1870, and died at Sand Fork of Kent in 1871. The original death-register image confirms Patrick's parents as **Luke & Margaret Murry**. The tax-list check now adds **Murry Eliza Jane** in 1853, plus image-verified **Murray James** and **Murry Patrick** in the same apparent 1854 district-list sequence. James appears on page 15 line 24 with one white male tithable and no listed property; Patrick appears on page 16 line 14 with one white male tithable and one horse valued at $50. The exact final year digit is cut off on the summary pages; `i3811884-00376`, `00374`, `00372`, `00370`, `00368`, `00366`, and `00364` were proof pages / proof spreads, and `i3811884-00375`, `00373`, `00371`, `00369`, `00367`, and `00365` were taxable-persons pages, none with a readable final year digit, district, or commissioner. Handle James and Patrick as probable 1854 until a cover/header page confirms the full year. No taxable **Luke / Lucas Murray / Murry** appears in the checked 1817-1855 M-sections. This supports a post-1850 Murry arrival or first tax appearance around 1852-1854 and still does not prove a pre-1843 Murray anchor family. The 1870 image work confirms rural Irish farming clusters in **Battelle** and **Willey**, with **Battelle image 8** now the strongest propertied cluster: **Bush / Leely / Mulooly / McDonald**. The district-geography pass still points the 1850-1870 rural cluster evidence toward the **Weston / courthouse hinterland** rather than directly toward Cove Lick / Camden / Loveberry. The next bounded Murray-specific work is checking `i3811884-00363` for a cover / header / year clue, tax-image capture and gap closure for 1855-1860, plus 1854 marriage/bond and 1852-1860 deed/court/probate checks.

## Research Question

Did Murray, Copley, Dolan, Hanley, Hannon, Gillooly, Reynolds, Mullooly, Mulroney, Mahon, or related Irish Catholic households cluster together in Lewis County before or near the 1843 Copley land purchase?

This matters because a census-neighbor cluster near Cove Lick, Camden, Loveberry, St. Michael's, or the Copley parcel would support the idea that the settlement was a coordinated Irish Catholic community rather than isolated family migration.

## Evaluation Rules

| Finding | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Murray household appears in Lewis County by 1840, near Copley / Camden / Loveberry / Catholic settlement surnames | Strong support for Murray anchor-family hypothesis. |
| Murray household appears by 1850 near Copley and settlement-family households | Moderate support; compare with deed evidence for earlier arrival. |
| Murray household appears in Lewis County but far from the Copley settlement zone | Partial support; requires mapping before changing conclusions. |
| No Murray household appears, but Murray deed evidence is confirmed | Census gap or temporary absence; deed evidence remains primary. |
| Murray households appear, but are entirely Virginia-born and not near the Irish-origin Copley household | Weak support for a Murray-centered Irish settlement in that census year; may indicate a separate Virginia Murray line. |
| No Murray household or deed evidence appears after variant searching | Weakens the anchor-family hypothesis; settlement name origin remains unresolved. |
| Munday / Monday / Mundy household appears near Copleys before 1850 | Reopens the Ann Munday surname question and requires direct-record review. |
