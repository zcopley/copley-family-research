RQ-M1 Lewis County FAN Sweep

This page is the findings log for census and neighbor-network research around the Murray Settlement hypothesis in Lewis County, Virginia / West Virginia.

Current status: The bounded Claude for Chrome FAN-sweep passes for 1850, 1860, and 1870 are now complete, and the key 1860 / 1870 cluster pages now have a direct image-capture pass, a Murry continuity pass, a vital-record verification pass, an original-image transcription pass, two older-Luke / Margaret indexed-search passes, a Lewis County personal-property-tax check, and a Luke Murray / Anne Loghan marriage-register image check. The 1850 pass found the Micheal / Patrick Coopley household in District 30, but no broader Irish surname cluster. The 1860 image capture confirms a dense Ireland-born cluster centered on Dolan / Murry / Tierney rural farming households, plus a separate Weston boarding-house / labor cluster around Loftus / Barret / Mc Mahan and other Ireland-born workers. The Murry continuity search shows Luke Murry moved from Lewis County to Providence, Rhode Island between late 1866 and March 1867, while the separate adult Patrick Murry stayed in Lewis County, appeared in Sheridan / Lewis in 1870, and died at Sand Fork of Kent in 1871. The original death-register image confirms Patrick’s parents as Luke & Margaret Murry. The tax-list check now has image-verified Murray James and Murry Patrick in the same confirmed 1854 district-list sequence; the earlier reported 1853 Eliza Jane row on i3811884-00262 has been corrected by native-tile reread to Merry, Eliza Jane, not Murry. The 1854 Loghan check confirms Loghan John and Loghan Owen on i3811884-00319, rows 15-16, in the C. John Ree / possible John C. Ree district; i3811884-00320 is the right-hand continuation / PROOF face of the same folded return and confirms 1854 for the Ree / Loghan list. Image i3811884-00321 continues the same Ree district into an M/Mc taxable-persons page and gives the strongest current orthographic support for the conservative John C. Ree / John Cs. Ree reading, i3811884-00322 is the paired abstract / proof page for 00321 and directly confirms 1854, i3811884-00323 continues the Ree district into M/N names, i3811884-00324 is another 1854 proof / abstract for the Ree district pages, i3811884-00325 continues the same Ree district into N/O names, i3811884-00326 is another 1854 proof / abstract most likely paired to 00325, i3811884-00327 continues the same John Ree district into P/R-type names, i3811884-00328 is the paired 1854 proof / abstract page likely closing the P/R return, i3811884-00329 continues the same John Ree district into R/S names, and i3811884-00330 is the paired 1854 proof / abstract page for 00329. These follow-ups add no Loghan, Murray / Murry, Donahoe / Donahue, or Kester names and no readable oath or commissioner signature. The i3811884-00362 proof / summary page confirms the James / Patrick year as 1854, and a bottom/signature recheck found no hidden signature, oath, affidavit, written commissioner name, or district label below the proof table. Kester subject to the faint-ink caveat; James remains tied by sequence. No taxable Luke / Lucas Murray / Murry or Margaret Murray / Murry appears in the checked or accessible 1817-1860 M-sections. The 1855-1860 gap check confirms James and Patrick again in 1857, confirms James in 1860, and adds Murray / Murrow Hudson, Murray Edwards, and Murray William as new FAN-club tax clues. The targeted cleanup confirms Murrow Hudson in 1859 and 1860, recovers his 1860 tax columns, and finds no Murray / Murry / Murrow / Murrey entry on the 1858 E. Slesher page 19. The WV Culture marriage-register image 825111/00184 confirms Luke Murray to Anne Loghan on 30 December 1854, issued by Jno Monroe Clk, but names no witnesses, bondsman, surety, parents, guardian, or officiant. The follow-up online bond / consent search found no remotely accessible separate bond, consent, or license return and showed Film 825112 / DGS 4130804 is register-only. This supports a post-1850 Murry arrival or first tax appearance by 1854 and still does not prove a pre-1843 Murray anchor family. The 1870 image work confirms rural Irish farming clusters in Battelle and Willey, with Battelle image 8 now the strongest propertied cluster: Bush / Leely / Mulooly / McDonald. The district-geography pass still points the 1850-1870 rural cluster evidence toward the Weston / courthouse hinterland rather than directly toward Cove Lick / Camden / Loveberry. The next high-value work is the restricted Smith compiled marriage-bonds index check, followed by 1852-1860 deed/court/probate checks; no additional bounded Ree follow-up is warranted from 00330 alone.

Research Question

Did Murray, Copley, Dolan, Hanley, Hannon, Gillooly, Reynolds, Mullooly, Mulroney, Mahon, or related Irish Catholic households cluster together in Lewis County before or near the 1843 Copley land purchase?

This matters because a census-neighbor cluster near Cove Lick, Camden, Loveberry, St. Michael’s, or the Copley parcel would support the idea that the settlement was a coordinated Irish Catholic community rather than isolated family migration.

Map check: M. Wood White’s 1875 county and district map shows Lewis County magisterial districts including Courthouse, Hackers Creek, Freemans Creek, Collins Settlement, and Skidmore, but it does not label Battelle Township or Willey under those names. The map supports a central Weston / Courthouse-Hackers Creek orientation but does not prove exact 1870 township boundaries.

Working interpretation: The 1850-1870 rural Irish farming evidence currently points most directly to the Weston / courthouse hinterland. It does not yet directly place the Battelle cluster in Cove Lick, Camden, or Loveberry. The relationship between those settlement place names, the 1843 Copley parcel, and the census district labels remains a map / land-record problem.

Evaluation Rules

FindingInterpretation
Murray household appears in Lewis County by 1840, near Copley / Camden / Loveberry / Catholic settlement surnamesStrong support for Murray anchor-family hypothesis.
Murray household appears by 1850 near Copley and settlement-family householdsModerate support; compare with deed evidence for earlier arrival.
Murray household appears in Lewis County but far from the Copley settlement zonePartial support; requires mapping before changing conclusions.
No Murray household appears, but Murray deed evidence is confirmedCensus gap or temporary absence; deed evidence remains primary.
Murray households appear, but are entirely Virginia-born and not near the Irish-origin Copley householdWeak support for a Murray-centered Irish settlement in that census year; may indicate a separate Virginia Murray line.
No Murray household or deed evidence appears after variant searchingWeakens the anchor-family hypothesis; settlement name origin remains unresolved.
