# RQ-C2 Ann Munday Origin Dashboard

## Question

What was [[People/Ann Copley|Ann Copley]]'s original surname and Irish origin, and should the project treat Munday or Murray as her working surname?

## Current Answer

Use **Munday** as the preferred recorded family surname. The **Murray** connection is now a downgraded variant hypothesis after Tom Copley's May 2026 correction, unless a direct record names Ann as Murray.

## Evidence Status

| Evidence | Status | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Family tradition / compiled materials | Supports Munday form | Working surname, but not original proof. |
| Griffith's Valuation Kinawley | Indirect | No Munday in Kinawley; multiple Murray occupiers. This supports variant testing, not identity proof. |
| Kinawley Catholic registers | Coverage gap | Ann's likely baptism predates available registers. |
| Tithe Applotment search | Inconclusive | Kinawley coverage gap; cannot prove either surname. |
| Tom Copley May 2026 correction | Important family-source correction | Downgrades Munday-to-Murray connection as probably mistaken. |

## Next Records

1. ORT-013: Direct surname record for Ann: marriage, passenger, child death, Bible, probate, cemetery, or obituary.
2. ORT-014: Kinawley / Clogher Catholic or collateral records for Michael Copley + Ann.
3. Search Munday / Monday / Mundy first; keep Murray / Murry only as a downgraded variant.

## Guardrail

Do not use the Murray Settlement framework as proof that Ann was a Murray. Settlement geography and surname clusters are context, not a direct identity record.
