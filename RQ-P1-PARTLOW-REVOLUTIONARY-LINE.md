# RQ-P1 Partlow Revolutionary Line

This page tracks the former proposed proof chain from [[People/Marion Elizabeth Partlow|Marion Elizabeth Partlow]] back to [[People/Benjamin Partlow|Benjamin Partlow]], the Revolutionary War pensioner in Culpeper / Rappahannock County, Virginia.

**Current status:** The downstream Partlow line from Marion Elizabeth back to **John H. / John Halleck Partlow** is supported by original or near-original web records. The proposed upstream link **Jacob Newton Partlow -> Benjamin Partlow** is now contradicted by a complete review of **Rappahannock County Chancery Case 1842-002 / original no. 53**. The full 69-image file names Benjamin Partlow's children and heirs after his 1837 intestate death, distributes the estate through eight child-lines, and **does not include Jacob / Jacob Newton / Nute Partlow** anywhere.

## Corrective Primary Record: Benjamin Partlow Heirs Chancery Case

The June 2026 primary-record search found **Rappahannock County Chancery Case 1842-002**, original case number **53**, indexed as **John Partlow v. Heir(s) of Benjamin Partlow** at the Library of Virginia. See [[References/Rappahannock Partlow Chancery Case 1842-002]].

Source:

- Repository: Library of Virginia Chancery Records Index.
- Case URL: https://old.lva.virginia.gov/chancery/case_detail.asp?CFN=157-1842-002
- Locality: Rappahannock County, Virginia.
- Images: 69 scanned images online.
- Folder / worksheet title: `Partlow vs. Partlow`, no. 53, 1840-1841.

This is almost certainly the "Culpeper County Chancery Papers #53" cited in earlier derivative literature. LVA indexes it under Rappahannock because Rappahannock County was formed from Culpeper County in 1833.

The bill of complaint states that Benjamin Partlow died intestate on **23 Dec 1837** and names the children and heirs as:

- John L. Partlow
- Elisha Partlow
- Sarah R. Morris
- Lucy Menefee
- Martha Behem
- Clarissa Cornwell
- the children of deceased daughter Catherine Menefee
- Thomas P. Bowran, son of deceased daughter Mary Bowran

**Jacob Partlow / Jacob Newton Partlow / Nute Partlow does not appear anywhere in the complete 69-image file.**

Interpretation: this is the best online primary record found so far for Benjamin Partlow's children. If Jacob Newton Partlow was alive until 1868, as the Clark County derivative line says, he should normally appear as a living child or heir if he were Benjamin's son. His absence from the full file materially changes the research conclusion. The derivative sources may be conflating two Benjamin Partlow families, placing Jacob under the wrong Benjamin, or skipping a generation.

The remaining images 5-69 have now been read. They confirm the eight-child structure through Benjamin's 1831 advancement schedule, eight-way land division decrees, commissioner's distribution reports, party lists, and sale report. The only Jacob is **Jacob Menefee**, a grandson through Lucy Partlow Menefee. Henry B. Partlow and Benjamin L. Partlow appear as land-sale purchasers, not heirs.

## 1883 Perrin County-History Passage: John H. to Jacob

The former "1888 Clark County history" lead has been resolved and corrected. The actual source is William Henry Perrin, ed., *History of Crawford and Clark Counties, Illinois* (Chicago: O. L. Baskin & Co., 1883), p. 138. See [[References/1883 Perrin Marion Partlow Biography]].

The biography of **Marion Partlow** states that Marion's father was **John H. Partlow**, born in 1811, and that John H. **"was a son of Jacob Partlow."** It also says John H. came from Indiana to Clark County in 1839 with wife **Lydia Bennett**, entered 80 acres on Section 35 in Johnson Township, and later died in Arkansas. The same passage identifies Marion's wife Martha L. Bowles and their children **Nollie Franklin**, Henry A., and Orie D.

Interpretation: this is strong near-contemporary local-history testimony for **John H. Partlow -> Jacob Partlow** and for the Marion / Nollie family frame. It does **not** mention Benjamin Partlow and cannot be used for **Jacob -> Benjamin**.

## Jacob Partlow Clark County Original Will

A later June 2026 probate search first located a derivative / snippet trail for **Jacob Partlow's will**. A follow-up search then found the original will-book entry. See [[References/Jacob Partlow Clark County Probate Will Lead]].

Citation and details:

- Jacob Partlow will, dated **3 Sep 1867**, proved in open court **26 May 1868**.
- Clark County, Illinois, Will Record Book 1, 1859-1882, pp. approximately 158-159, image 280 of 1338.
- Ancestry collection 9048, record `1196850`, image `007655352_00280`.
- FamilySearch DGS `7655352` / Film `1308123`, catalog `koha:317736`.
- Wife **Polly** receives a life estate in all remaining property.
- The will says Jacob had already given **all lawful heirs $50 each**.
- **William** receives the residue after Polly's life estate, but the will does not identify him as William Franklin Partlow.
- **Polly Ritenhouse / Ridenhouse** receives a cow and a bed; her relationship is not stated.
- Executor: **William W. Willison**.
- Witnesses: **Jacob Geffs** and **Roger S. Davis**.

Interpretation: the will-book entry is now an original-record anchor, but it still does **not** name John H. Partlow, does **not** name all heirs, does **not** identify William by middle name, and does **not** explain Polly Ritenhouse / Ridenhouse. The next target is the separate probate packet, docket, executor bond, inventory, sale bill, settlement, and distribution receipts, if they survive.

The probate-index follow-up found Jacob in the Clark County `INDEX TO ESTATE PAPERS` at Ancestry collection 9048 record `1196688`, image `007655352_00192`. The entries are **`PARTLOW Jacob - (CC) 166`** and **`PARTLOW Jacob - 188`**. The `188` entry points to the will-book page already found; `(CC) 166` is a new unresolved court / county-court lead. The same index page includes **`PARTLOW John M. - 1/28`**, which should not be assumed to be John H. until checked. The packet search found no online executor bond, letters, inventory, sale bill, final settlement, receipt, distribution, or guardianship record in the checked image ranges.

The same companion genealogy snippet reportedly separates Jacob's marriages into **Mary Halleck** about 1810, with children John Halleck Partlow and James Halleck Partlow III, and **Mary / Polly Abrams** in 1815 in Indiana. Treat this as a working compiled-source structure until the original Mary Halleck evidence is found. The indexed 1815 Polly Abrams marriage remains the stronger direct marriage record.
