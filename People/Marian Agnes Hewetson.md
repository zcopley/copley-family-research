# Marian Agnes Hewetson

## Biographical Profile

[[Marian Agnes Hewetson]] is identified by Tom Copley as [[Barbara Dee LaForce|Dee LaForce]]'s grandmother, known in family speech as **Grammy**. Tom's June 2026 correction identifies her as Maggie's mother and the woman seated between [[Marion Elizabeth Partlow]] and [[Michael Joseph Copley]] in the likely LaForce Christmas-party photograph from about 1967-1968.

Her first name was spelled **Marian** with an `a`, unlike [[Marion Elizabeth Partlow|Marion Copley]], whose name used an `o`.

## Family Relationships

- **Daughter:** Maggie, per Tom Copley's June 2026 correction.
- **Granddaughter:** [[Barbara Dee LaForce]], per Tom Copley's June 2026 correction.

## Notes

- The preserved photo identification is family testimony, not an independently dated or formally labeled photograph.
- The photo context is likely a LaForce Christmas party, probably 1967-1968.
