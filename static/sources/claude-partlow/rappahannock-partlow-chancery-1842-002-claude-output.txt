This is the "Culpeper County Chancery Papers #53, ca. 1840-1841" cited in genealogical literature. The case is in fact a Rappahannock County record (Rappahannock was carved from Culpeper in 1833), filed 1840-41, with original case number 53. Researchers who cite it as "Culpeper" are relying on the pre-partition location. LVA correctly assigns it to Rappahannock. Originals are at the Rappahannock County Circuit Court Clerk's Office; LVA has digital scans.

Final Conclusion

The best primary-record source found online - Rappahannock County Chancery Case 1842-002 (original case number 53, filed 1840-41), accessible in full as 69 scanned images at https://old.lva.virginia.gov/chancery/case_detail.asp?CFN=157-1842-002 - directly and exhaustively names all children and heirs of Benjamin Partlow (d. intestate 23 Dec 1837).

The complete list of Benjamin Partlow's children/heirs as stated in the Bill of Complaint (image 3) is:
John L. Partlow; Elisha Partlow; Sarah R. Morris (nee Partlow); Lucy Menefee (nee Partlow); Martha Behem (nee Partlow); Clarissa Cornwell (nee Partlow); Catherine Menefee (deceased daughter, represented by her children); Mary Bowran (deceased daughter, represented by Thomas P. Bowran)

Jacob Newton Partlow, Jacob Partlow, and "Nute" Partlow do NOT appear anywhere in this case.

This is the strongest online primary record available for identifying Benjamin Partlow's children. It does not prove Jacob as Benjamin's child; in fact, it affirmatively omits Jacob from a sworn Bill of Complaint naming the complete heir class.
