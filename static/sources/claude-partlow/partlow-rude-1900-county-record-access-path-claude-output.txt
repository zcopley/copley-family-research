Research Report: Frank Partlow / Alice Rude Marriage, Clark County, Illinois, June 1900

Summary Finding

No original, reconstructed, or derivative county record for this marriage has been located in any online repository. The marriage license issued 8 June 1900 appears to fall in a documented gap between the end of the FamilySearch microfilm coverage (Vol. H, through 1899) and the post-fire survival record. The record may still survive at the Clark County Clerk's office, or at the Marshall Public Library's Genealogy Collection, but requires a direct inquiry to establish.

This is a derivative record, a newspaper notice of license issuance, not a ceremony return.

Whether Post-1899 Clark County Marriage Records Survive

Possibly, but with caveats. The IRAD documentation states that the 30 December 1902 courthouse fire caused "some records lost or damaged," not a total loss. The marriage register Vol. H covered 1890-1899 and was fully filmed before the fire. Whether a Vol. I (which would cover 1900 onward) was created and survived the fire, or was among records damaged, is unknown from online sources. The online records portal for Clark County offers Genealogy Marriage Certificates for records 75+ years old, implying the county holds some older records, but whether it has the 1900 book requires direct contact. The FamilySearch / ISGS index includes only 14 Partlow entries in Clark County, the latest dated June 1897 (Vol. H, p. 422), suggesting that indexers either did not have access to records beyond that point or no marriage register for 1900 was available to them at the time of indexing.

Most likely scenarios:
- A marriage register Vol. I for 1900-1902 may have survived the 1902 fire.
- It may have been partially damaged or lost.
- The Clark County Genealogical Society's "Extract of marriages...1819-1900" publication at the Marshall Public Library may include it if compilers had pre-fire access to the records.

What Was Not Found

No original marriage license, license return, marriage register entry, application, affidavit, or reconstructed record for Frank Partlow / Alice Rude, about 8-10 June 1900, Clark County, IL, exists in any online database searched. The newspaper notice proves the license was issued 8 June 1900 but does not constitute proof of ceremony.

Distinction: License Issued vs. Marriage Ceremony Proven

Clark County Herald, 14 Jun 1900, p. 4: newspaper notice / derivative; proves a marriage license was issued to Frank Partlow, 25, and Alice Rude, 19, on 8 June 1900 in Clark County, IL.
No original county record found: the ceremony date, officiant, witnesses, and return of license are not yet proven from any surviving original or reconstructed record.
1900 federal census, June 1900, Johnson Twp.: primary / original census evidence placing Frank N. Partlow and Alice in Clark County around the time; consistent with a June 1900 marriage but does not itself document the ceremony.

The newspaper notice is solid corroborating evidence of license issuance. Genealogical proof of the marriage date and ceremony requires finding the license return, register entry, or another official record. That record, if it survives, is most likely held by the Clark County Clerk's office or the Marshall Public Library genealogy collection.
