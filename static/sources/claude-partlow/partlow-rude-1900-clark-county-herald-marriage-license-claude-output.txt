Evidentiary assessment:

This notice confirms that a Clark County marriage license was issued on June 8, 1900, for Frank Partlow, age 25, of Moonshine, and Alice Rude, age 19. It strongly supports the reported marriage around June 10, 1900 and helps resolve the bride's working name variant toward Alice Rude in this local-license context. It is a license notice, not a ceremony report, and does not by itself give the exact ceremony date, officiant, witnesses, parents, or original license number.
