1900 U.S. Federal Census Search Results: Partlow & Rude, Clark County, Illinois

Result:

Frank N. Partlow and Mary A. Rude were both found in Johnson Township, Clark County, Illinois, in the 1900 federal census. They do not appear together as a married couple. Both are enumerated as single, consistent with the 1900 census reference date of 1 June 1900 and a reported marriage date of 10 June 1900.

Interpretation:

The census does not prove the 10 June 1900 marriage because it uses a 1 June 1900 census reference date. If Frank / Nollie Partlow and Mary Alice Rude married on 10 June 1900, both should still appear as single on the census reference date. The finding is therefore consistent with the reported marriage, but does not corroborate the marriage directly.

The result does establish important circumstantial context:
- Both were living in Johnson Township, Clark County, Illinois, in the same enumeration district.
- Both were recorded as single on the 1 June 1900 reference date.
- Frank N Partlow was age 25, born Apr 1875 in Illinois.
- Mary A Rude was age 20, born Oct 1880 in Illinois.
- They were enumerated on adjacent sides of the same physical census sheet: Frank on sheet 7A, Mary A. Rude on sheet 7B.
