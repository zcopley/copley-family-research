The Publication: What It Is and Where It Physically Exists

FamilySearch Catalog record: familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:279795

Full title: Extract of marriages from Clark County, 1819-1900.
Author/compiler: Clark County Genealogical Society, Marshall, Illinois.
Format: Book (3 volumes) + Microfilm 35 mm.
Date of publication: not formally identified; based on other references, compiled circa 1975.
Contents:
- v. 1: 1819-1851
- v. 2: 1851-1881
- v. 3: 1881-1900. This is the volume containing any June 1900 entry.
Includes index.
Physical copies held at: FamilySearch Library, Salt Lake City, 3rd Floor Books, call numbers 977.371 V25c v. 1-2 and v. 3, listed as Available.
Microfilm: Film Number 1036682, Items 6-8; Digital Group Number (DGS) 7955859; filmed by the Genealogical Society of Utah in 1978 on 1 reel, 35 mm. Also held at FamilySearch Library, U.S. & Canada B1 Floor Film.
Online status: no camera icon; not available for online viewing.

The Marshall Public Library's own catalog does not show a copy. However, the library's Genealogy FAQ states they hold "300+ volumes of county records" received from the former Clark County Genealogical Library, so a physical copy likely exists in their on-site genealogy room even if not in the public ILS.

Online/Searchable Copy Status

No online or remotely searchable copy exists. The publication is not on Internet Archive, HathiTrust, or Google Books; it is not accessible via FamilySearch's digital image viewer; and no verifiable OCLC number surfaced via WorldCat / Google.

The only confirmed access route is the FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City in person, or a lookup request from Marshall Public Library if their genealogy room holds the physical copy.

No Partlow-Rude Entry Found Online

No transcript or citation for Frank Partlow / Alice Rude has been located in any searchable online database. The only current documentary record of the couple's marriage activity is:

Clark County Herald, Marshall, Illinois, 14 June 1900, page 4, Marriage Licenses column, under "June 8-": "Frank Partlow, 25, Moonshine." / "Alice Rude, 19."

Critical Distinction: License Notice vs. Ceremony Proof

Clark County Herald, 14 Jun 1900: proves a license was applied for / issued on June 8, 1900; notes ages and groom's township; does not prove ceremony, officiant, or return date.
FamilySearch Collection 1803970: only covers Clark County marriages through 1899; proves nothing about the 1900 event.
Extract of marriages, v. 3 (1881-1900), unverified for Partlow: would be a compiled extraction from county record volumes; completeness is unknown. County films stop at 1899, so the extract may also stop there or may have drawn from a physical ledger.
MyHeritage entry, derivative: derivative of the newspaper notice, not independent primary evidence.

The courthouse fire of 30 December 1902 adds uncertainty. If the original marriage return ledger covering 1900-1902 was damaged or destroyed, there may be no surviving official ceremony record, only the license newspaper notice.

Summary

The Extract of marriages from Clark County, 1819-1900 (Clark County Genealogical Society, Marshall, IL) is a real confirmed publication in 3 volumes. Volume 3 (1881-1900) is relevant to the June 1900 Partlow-Rude license. The FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City holds both the physical books and a microfilm copy (Film 1036682 / DGS 7955859), but the content is not available online. The Marshall Public Library's online catalog does not list it, though the library likely holds a physical copy in the on-site genealogy room since it inherited the Clark County Genealogical Library's collection. No online source contains a Partlow-Rude marriage entry. The newspaper notice proves only that a license was issued on June 8, 1900. It does not constitute proof of ceremony or legal return. The 1902 courthouse fire adds plausible grounds for a missing return record but cannot be confirmed without checking local holdings.
