Complete Research Report: The 1883 Clark County, Illinois County History - Partlow Passage

Source located:
William Henry Perrin, editor, History of Crawford and Clark Counties, Illinois (Chicago: O. L. Baskin & Co., Historical Publishers, 1883), page 138, Johnson Township biography of Marion Partlow.

Internet Archive identifier: historyofcrawfor00perr
Page URL: https://archive.org/details/historyofcrawfor00perr/page/138/mode/2up?q=Partlow
OCR text: https://archive.org/stream/historyofcrawfor00perr/historyofcrawfor00perr_djvu.txt

Important correction:
The source is the 1883 Perrin county history, not an 1888 history. A later social-media post or snippet trail appears to have mislabeled the date.

Core transcript / abstract:
The biography begins "MARION PARTLOW, farmer, P. O. Bell Air." It says the Partlow family came to Clark County in fall 1839. It identifies Marion's father as John H. Partlow, born in 1811 in one of the Middle States, and explicitly says John H. "was a son of Jacob Partlow." John H. came from Indiana to Clark County in 1839 with wife Lydia, entered 80 acres in Section 35 of Johnson Township, remained there for many years, later went to Arkansas for his health, and died there. His wife was Lydia Bennett before marriage and bore him seventeen children; six were living when the sketch was written: Marion, Columbus N., and four sisters.

The Marion paragraph says Marion was born 27 Feb 1844 on the farm he owned, married Martha L. Bowles, daughter of Wilson Bowles, and Martha died in March 1881 leaving three children: Nollie Franklin, Henry A., and Orie D.

Evidence assessment:
The passage directly states John H. Partlow was a son of Jacob Partlow. It directly identifies Marion Partlow as son of John H. and Lydia Bennett and gives a local family account that fits the census chain. It also names Nollie Franklin as a child of Marion and Martha L. Bowles.

Benjamin Partlow is not mentioned in this passage. A full-text search did not find a Partlow-related Benjamin claim in this source. The 1883 Perrin source therefore supports John H. -> Jacob, but it does not support Jacob -> Benjamin.

Source type:
County-history / local-biographical sketch, probably based on information supplied by Marion Partlow or close relatives around 1882-1883. Use as strong near-contemporary family testimony, not as primary probate or vital evidence.

Research consequence:
The old task to find the "1888 History of Clark County" page is complete and corrected to 1883 Perrin, page 138. The new targets are Jacob Partlow's probate / estate file, the original Indiana marriage register for Jacob Partlow and Polly Abrams, and direct census / Abrams follow-up.
