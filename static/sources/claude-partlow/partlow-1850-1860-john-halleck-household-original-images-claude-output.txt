1850 Interpretation

The original image strongly supports Marion McDonald Partlow as a child of John H. Partlow and Lydia Bennett Partlow. The child's written given name is difficult, with the best defensible reading Momon and the FamilySearch index reading Momore. New England Ball Project also noted Momon. The age, sex, birthplace, household, and later 1860 Marion M. record identify this child as Marion McDonald Partlow.

Nearby Partlow household: Isaac Partlow appears seven pages earlier, image 302 / page 543, as a nearby relation. No other Partlow household appears on page 550 itself.

1860 Interpretation

The original image conclusively supports Marion McDonald Partlow as a child in the John Partlow / Lydia household. The name reads clearly as Marion M, age 16, born Illinois, in Johnson Township, Clark County, Illinois.

Nearby Partlow household: Benjamin Partlow and Sarah An Partlow appear on the same page as a separate household immediately following John Partlow's household.

Discrepancies and Notes

- 1860 page number: New England Ball Project cited p. 65, but the original image and FamilySearch panel show page 135. This may be a different pagination system or a citation error.
- 1850 surname indexing: FamilySearch misindexed the household as Partton; the image reads Partlow.
- 1850 Marion given name: FamilySearch reads Momore; NEBP noted Momon. The original handwriting is difficult. The most defensible reading is Momon, an unusual rendering that is identified as Marion by household continuity and the 1860 Marion M entry.
- Marion sex: both census records index Marion as male, consistent with Marion McDonald Partlow, 1844-1922.
