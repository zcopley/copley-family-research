Jacob Partlow -- Clark County Probate Follow-Up Research Report

Session focus: verify the meaning of probate-index entry "PARTLOW Jacob - (CC) 166"; determine whether Probate Journal volume CC is online; check the separate "PARTLOW John M. - 1/28" lead; search 1868-1872 Clark County / Marshall newspapers.

Key findings:

- The 1938 Inventory of County Archives of Illinois, No. 12 -- Clark County (Marshall), entry 127, identifies the Probate Journal series, 1820-, also titled Probate Order Book or Probate Record.
- Entry 127 describes a 43-volume series: numbered volumes 2-5, two unlabeled volumes, volume B, then the A-Z / later continuation sequence beginning 1860.
- The probate-index notation "(CC) 166" is interpreted as Clark County Probate Journal / Probate Order Book volume CC, page 166.
- Probate Journal volume CC is not included in the seven-reel FamilySearch / Ancestry set cataloged as FamilySearch koha:317736 / Ancestry collection 9048.
- The seven reels include probate indexes, will records, guardian records, conservator records, administrator records, and some chancery books, but not the Probate Journal / Probate Order Book volume CC.
- Therefore the underlying CC 166 entry requires Clark County courthouse or IRAD-EIU access unless a separate digitization is found.

- This supplies a physical estate-packet pointer: Jacob Partlow estate file box 46.
- Right-column Partlow entries include possible John H. / John M. readings at higher box numbers, but the image was not resolved conclusively.

John M. / 1/28:

- The "PARTLOW John M. - 1/28" notation uses a format different from the box-number style in the Index to Estate Papers.
- It may refer to a volume/page in another probate or administrator-guardian index.
- DGS 7655347 contains the Administrator-Guardian Index 1876-1893 and is online through Ancestry collection 9048; this remains the best next online target.
- Do not equate John M. with John H. unless the underlying record proves it.

Newspaper searches:

  - Ritenhouse / Ridenhouse, Jacob Geffs, Roger S. Davis, and phrase "Partlow estate": no relevant hits.
