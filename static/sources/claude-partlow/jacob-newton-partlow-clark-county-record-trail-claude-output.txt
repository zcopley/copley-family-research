Core answers:
No primary record found names Jacob's parents. The only parent claim in this record set is Find a Grave's Benjamin field, which is derivative and contradicted by the complete Rappahannock chancery file for Benjamin Partlow's heirs.

Mary Halleck vs. Mary Abrams: the retrieved record evidence strongly favors Mary / Polly Abrams. The 13 Sep 1815 Indiana marriage record names Polly Abrams and Jacob Partlow. No retrieved record supports Mary Halleck.

John H. as Jacob's son: no primary record retrieved here directly names John H. as Jacob's son. The relationship is supported by the 1883 Perrin county-history biography, local proximity, 1840 / 1850 / 1860 census continuity, and compiled family sources. Jacob's probate remains the most important next proof target.

Best next targets:
1. Clark County, Illinois probate records for Jacob Partlow, circa 1868-1875.
2. Original Indiana marriage register for Jacob Partlow and Polly Abrams, 13 Sep 1815, FHL film 000549348.
3. Direct review of the 1860 census image for Jacob / Mary / William Partlow.
4. 1850 census browse for Jacob Partlow near Johnson Township / John H. Partlow.
5. 1870 census for Mary Partlow as probable widow.
6. Abrams family records in Indiana, Kentucky, and Ohio.
