Research Report: Frank N. / Nollie Franklin Partlow & Alice / Mary Alice Rude Partlow

Summary of Findings

The research produced a strong, multi-source chain of evidence connecting Frank / Nollie Partlow, Alice / Mary Alice Rude Partlow, and Marion Elizabeth Partlow Copley across every decade from 1900 to 1956. The key identities are proved for Frank / Nollie and proved by convergent evidence for Alice / Mary Alice. Marion Elizabeth's parentage is confirmed by the 1920 and 1930 census records and supported by Find a Grave memorial links.

Assessment:

Frank Partlow = Nollie Franklin Partlow: PROVEN. The identity is established by matching name variants, age, place, family grouping, later attached records, the Find a Grave memorial title, and the obituary that uses the everyday name Frank while the memorial supplies Nollie Franklin.

Alice Rude = Mary Alice Rude Partlow: PROVEN by convergent evidence. The evidence chain runs from Mary A Rude in 1900 to Alice Rude in the 8 Jun 1900 marriage-license notice, Alice Partlow in 1910/1940/1950, Mary A Parttow in 1920, Allee / Alice Partlow in 1930, and Alice Rude as the mother of Ray Partlow in the 1934 Indiana marriage record.

Marion Elizabeth Partlow is the daughter of Frank / Nollie Partlow and Alice / Mary Alice Rude Partlow: PROVEN by near-original census records. The 1920 census lists Marian E in the household of Nollie / Mary A Partlow, and the 1930 census lists Marion Partlow explicitly as daughter of Frank and Allee / Alice Partlow.

What was not found online:
- A standalone open Find a Grave memorial page for Alice / Mary Alice Rude Partlow.
- Online Indiana or Illinois death certificates for Frank or Alice.
- A WWI draft-registration record for Frank / Nollie.
- An online obituary for Alice / Mary Alice Partlow.
- An original or reconstructed Clark County marriage license return / ceremony record for Frank Partlow and Alice Rude. The 14 Jun 1900 Clark County Herald notice proves license issuance, not the ceremony return.
