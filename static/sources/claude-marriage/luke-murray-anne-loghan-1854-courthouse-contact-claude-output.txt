Notes on Correct Office

In Virginia and early West Virginia, marriage bonds were executed before the Clerk of the County Court. When West Virginia organized its courts, the Circuit Court absorbed the old county-court records. The WV State Archives confirms that county records remained with the counties; the Archives holds only microfilm copies. The Circuit Clerk is therefore the first and correct point of contact for original marriage bonds and bond books. The County Clerk is a reasonable secondary contact for any bond records that may have migrated with the marriage-license function.

Fee / Copy Instructions

WV State Archives reference if the county refers the request there:
- Out-of-state copy request, uncertified county record: $8.00 per request.
- In-state: $5.00.
- Requests must be submitted in writing only, no email, to: Archives and History Library, 1900 Kanawha Blvd East, Bldg 9, Charleston, WV 25305-0300.
- Source: https://archive.wvculture.org/history/archives/services.html

Individual county offices set their own copy fees separately; ask the Circuit Clerk what they charge per page. Plain copies are often roughly $0.25-$1.00 per page; certified copies are often roughly $1.00-$2.00.

Re: Records Search - Marriage Bond/Consent, Luke Murray and Anne Loghan, ca. December 1854

Dear Ms. Burkhart,

I am conducting genealogical research on a Lewis County, Virginia, now West Virginia, marriage and write to ask whether any original marriage-bond or consent paper survives in your office's holdings.

The marriage of record:

Groom: Luke Murray
Bride: Anne Loghan, also found as Logan or Lohan
Date: 30 December 1854
County: Lewis County, Virginia, now West Virginia
Register citation: Lewis County Marriage Register, page 75
License issuer per register: Jno Monroe, Clerk

This entry appears in the marriage register that was microfilmed by the Genealogical Society of Utah, WV Culture Film 825111, Image 00184; FamilySearch DGS 4130803. The register records the date and parties but does not name a bondsman, surety, consent giver, witnesses, or officiant. I have searched the available online index paths, and negative results leave the original bond papers, bond books, or consent documents as the only untapped source.

I have two specific requests:

1. Does the Circuit Clerk's office hold any original marriage-bond paper, surety document, consent certificate, or bond-book entry for Luke Murray and Anne Loghan, executed on or about 30 December 1854? Please also check under spelling variants: Murray / Murry / Murrey and Loghan / Logan / Lohan.

2. Does your office hold a series of Lewis County marriage-bond books, sometimes numbered or labeled as bond books or bond registers, that cover December 1854? Specifically, I would like to know whether bond books 5, 6, 7, or 8, or any volume in a sequence covering that period, survive and could be examined or copied for the December 1854 date range.

If the records exist, I would be grateful for a photocopy. Please let me know the applicable fee per page or per record and the preferred payment method, and I will respond promptly.

If the Circuit Clerk's office does not hold these records, could you kindly advise whether they were transferred to the Lewis County Clerk, the West Virginia State Archives, or another repository?

Thank you very much for your time and assistance.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]
