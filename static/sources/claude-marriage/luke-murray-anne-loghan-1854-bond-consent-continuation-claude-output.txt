### Interpretation

**No separate 1854 marriage bond, consent paper, or license return for Luke Murray and Anne Loghan exists in any remotely accessible online source.** The "Marriage Consent" label on ARK 1:1:FRRG-HPT is a FamilySearch indexer classification of the register entry — not evidence of a scanned consent document. There is only one distinct image record for this marriage in all databases checked, and it is the known license-register page.

The FamilySearch "West Virginia, Marriages, 1780-1970" collection indexed **both** the license-register entry (ARK Q2ZX-G78G, image unavailable on FS) and the same record via the WV Culture VRR database (ARK FRRG-HPT, "Marriage Consent," routes to wvculture.org Image 184). These are **two index records for the same physical page**, not two different documents.

### What Remains Inaccessible and How to Get It

The only path to potential bondsman/witness/parent data is the **Smith compiled index**, which covers Lewis County marriage bonds 1816–1865 and explicitly records bondsmen, parents' names (where given), and bond-book page numbers. Two access options:

1. **In person at the FamilySearch Library, Salt Lake City:** Physical book, call number **975.461 V28s**, 3rd Floor (confirmed available). Look under "M" in the groom index for Murray, Luke. Takes only minutes on-site.

2. **At any FamilySearch Center or affiliate library:** Film 1598435 Item 6 / **DGS 7949996**. The film must be ordered to the center if not already in their permanent collection. Images are restricted from home viewing.

3. **Lewis County Courthouse, Weston, WV:** The original Lewis County marriage bond book (covering 1816–1865) is held there. The Smith index, if it lists Murray/Loghan, will give the specific book and page number to request from the county clerk.
