RQ-M1 INTERPRETATION

Does the Smith index identify a bondsman, surety, parent, bond book, or page for Luke / Anne?

No. Neither the groom index under MURRAY / MURRY / MURREY nor the bride index under LOGHAN / LOGAN / LOHAN contains any entry for this couple. The Smith index is silent on the 1854 Luke Murray / Anne Loghan marriage.

Does it connect Luke / Anne to John Loghan, Owen Loghan, James Murray, Patrick Murry, Donahoe / Donahue, C. Kester, or the Ree district?

No direct connection was found. The one Murray-related entry in the index, a Mulkey groom with parents Jonathan and Sarah Murray in 1849, does not name Luke Murray and involves different people. No Loghan parent or relative entry was found anywhere in the bride index L section.

Does this change the post-1850 first-tax-appearance model or older Luke / Margaret interpretation?

The Smith index result is a confirmatory negative, not a change. It confirms that no original bond was recovered in this compiled secondary source. The absence from Smith does not prove that no bond was ever filed; it means either the bond was filed but Smith did not locate or transcribe it, the original bond paper is lost or was never separated into a findable category, or Smith's index had gaps. Lewis County bond papers from the 1854 period are not all preserved, and Smith's index was compiled from records available circa 1991 and may not include all surviving or once-surviving bonds.

What exact courthouse request or image, if any, should be pursued next?

The one actionable next step that remains open is a direct written request to the Lewis County Circuit Clerk's Office, 110 Center Avenue, Weston, West Virginia 26452. The request should cite:
- Marriage of Luke Murray to Anne Loghan, dated 30 December 1854.
- Known register location: Lewis County Marriage Register page 75, WV Culture Film 825111, Image 00184.
- Request: any surviving marriage bond, consent, or surety document for this couple from Book 5, 6, 7, or 8 of the Lewis County bond records. The Smith index uses books numbered 5-8 for mid-1840s through 1865 entries.
- Ask specifically whether the bond books themselves survive and whether any bond for Murray / Loghan, December 1854, can be located.

The Smith index's silence does not rule out survival of the original paper. Lewis County bond records are partially extant, and some may survive in the courthouse that Smith did not capture. The courthouse inquiry is the only remaining access route not yet exhausted by remote search.
