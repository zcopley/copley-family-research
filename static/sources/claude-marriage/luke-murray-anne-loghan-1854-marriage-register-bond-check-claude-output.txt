Full Transcription of the Luke Murray / Anne Loghan Entry

Source: WV Culture, Film 825111, Image 00184 - Lewis County Marriage Register, Volume 2-4 (1833-1857), Register Page 75.

The entry is the second of four entries on page 75 of the register, in the sequence: Dean / Simpson (Dec 26, 1854) -> Murray / Loghan (Dec 30, 1854) -> Hite / Smith (Jan 3, 1855) -> Brown / Moneypenny (May 12, 1855).

Verbatim transcription:

Virginia / Lewis County Ct. [Clk.] / These are to license and permit you to join in the Holy Estate of Matrimony Luke Murray to Anne Loghan And for so doing this shall be your authority. Given when my hand this 30th day of Dec 1854 and 79th Year of the Commonwealth. Jno Monroe Clk

Witnesses, Bondsmen, Officiant, Parents, Consent Language

- Officiant: Not named in the register entry. The license authorizes any person authorized to solemnize the rites of matrimony in this state.
- Witnesses: None named in the register entry.
- Bondsman / Surety: None named in the register entry.
- Parents / Guardian: None named in the register entry.
- Consent language: Standard license boilerplate.
- Clerk issuing license: Jno / John Monroe, Clerk of Lewis County Court.
- Left-margin checkmarks: Two check marks appear in the left margin alongside the entry, consistent with the register's practice for other entries.

The register records issuance of a license, not the solemnization or its return. No witnesses, bondsman, surety, or parental consent language is preserved on this page. Whether the bond / consent document is on a separate film frame, possibly adjacent frames on the same or a companion reel, could not be confirmed in this pass.

Exact Bride Surname Spelling

- "Loghan" - as written in the register image and as indexed in FamilySearch ARK Q2ZX-G78G.
- "Log?an" - as indexed in both WV Culture VRR and FamilySearch ARK FRRG-HPT.

The original handwriting, read directly from image 00184, clearly supports Loghan. The WV Culture indexer's uncertainty about a single letter is understandable given the script style, but the direct image reading is confident.

What Image to Check Next

1. Determine whether a separate bond / consent paper was filmed on adjacent frames of Film 825111 or a companion film. Lewis County bond books, typically 1817-1850s, may be on a separate reel. The FamilySearch catalog for DGS 4130803 covers only volumes 2-4 (1833-1857) of the marriage register. Bond records, if filmed, may be on a different volume or a separately cataloged item.
2. Check the FamilySearch catalog for Lewis County, WV under marriage bonds. A separate catalog item may exist for Lewis County marriage bonds.
3. Film 825112 (DGS 4130804) contains "Marriages, v. 1 (1817-1833) and v. 5-7 (1853-1895)." The overlap with the 1854 date is suspicious and may contain a parallel series, possibly index or bond books. This is the highest-priority alternate film to check.
4. If bonds are not on 825111 / 825112, they may be unfilmed and held only at the Lewis County Courthouse in Weston, West Virginia, where the originals reside per the FamilySearch catalog note.
5. The FamilySearch collection "West Virginia, County Marriage Records, 1776-1971" appears to be a separate digitization effort that may have images for bond / consent pages that were not available when the record was indexed. That collection's image may become available in the future or may require a FamilySearch center visit.
