## Interpretation

The marginal note on Book 11, page 321 remains meaningful but unresolved. The word OCR-read as "Old" may be a local abbreviation, a difficult rendering of another instrument abbreviation such as quitclaim / release / agreement, or a non-deed reference. Because the deed indexes and the checked DB 16 / DB 17 areas do not produce a matching instrument, the note should no longer be treated as a straightforward Lewis County deed-book lookup.
