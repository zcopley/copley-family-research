## Relationship to the 1900 Exchange Deed

The 22 August 1900 Copley family exchange deed cites an older deed as source of title. The source deed is now confirmed as Deed Book 11, page 321. The previous "page 32?" reading was a page-number misread, likely because the final "1" in 321 was abbreviated, cramped, or lost in the image.

The 1 May 1880 deed conveys from Hoffman heirs / representatives to Michael Copley and John Copley. It describes 200 acres on Land Fork, bounded by Sand Fork and Cove Lick, from the upper part of Weeden Hoffman's 500-acre survey and within the Pickering 5,000-acre survey envelope. This is the Hoffman-Copley land base. The consideration language indicates the $500 purchase price had already been paid.

The 1900 exchange deed then redistributed interests among surviving Copley heirs. A marginal notation on image 193 is meaningful but gives no book/page. OCR reads "Old to John Copley March 21 . 1886," suggesting a possible later conveyance, release, quitclaim, assignment, or non-deed reference involving Michael Copley's interest and John Copley. A later deed-index follow-up did not locate a matching instrument in the checked Lewis County deed-book paths.
