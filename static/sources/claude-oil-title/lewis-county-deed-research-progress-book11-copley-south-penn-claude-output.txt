Research Progress Report - Lewis County Deed Research, Copley Family

Research date: June 2026
Preservation note: normalized extract of the user-supplied Claude for Chrome progress report on the August 1900 Copley exchange deed citation, Deed Book 11 page check, grantor-index search, and South Penn lease status.

PART 1: EXCHANGE DEED CITATION VERIFICATION

The exchange deed at DGS 008293301, image 245, Image ARK 3Q9M-CSR7-HQCR-V, remains confirmed as a family exchange deed, not an oil lease. It was executed 22 August 1900 and recorded 13 October 1900.

The latest tile-level pass revises the older deed citation:

- cited deed date reads May first, 1880, not May first, 1878;
- book reads Deed Book No. 11;
- page reads 32 with reasonable confidence, but a third digit cannot be ruled out from tile-boundary evidence;
- the 1900 exchange-deed place reading was reported in this pass as "Nonypod Run of Sand Creek," replacing the prior tentative "Bungport Run of Cove Lick" reading. Treat the place-name reading as a transcription conflict until the full image is reread.

PART 2: DEED BOOK 11 PAGE 32 CHECK

DGS 008293286, Film 818759, is Deed Book 11, years 1880-1881.

Page 32 was checked at image 47. The left page includes:

1. acknowledgment of a prior deed involving Harrison County / Crisell or related parties;
2. a recording endorsement by clerk Josh J. Wofter;
3. the start of a Franklin Maxwell deed at the bottom of the page.

No Copley deed was found at Deed Book 11 page 32.

Interpretive possibilities:

- page 32 may be a misread or partial read of another page number, such as 52, 62, 82, or 322;
- the relevant Copley deed may begin later in the page range;
- the citation may refer to a different type of entry.

PART 3: GRANTOR INDEX SEARCH

DGS 008219260, Film 819917, is the General Index to Deeds and Oil & Gas Leases, Lewis County, West Virginia, Grantors A-E, 1817-1934. It explicitly includes oil and gas leases.

The Co- section was located around images 304-306. Image 305 contains Colvin entries on the left page and Chandler entries on the right page. Image 306 begins Cork entries. No Copley / Coply / Copely / Copeley / Coopley entries were found in the visible boundary between Colvin and Cork, but this needs exact tile-boundary verification at images 305-306 and nearby pages 304-310.

South Penn Oil Company entries for other grantors were observed, including a George A. Brown / Mary E. Brown lease to South Penn Oil Co. for 300 acres in Lewis and Doddridge counties.

PART 4: SOUTH PENN LEASE STATUS

The South Penn / Copley heirs lease has not yet been located.
