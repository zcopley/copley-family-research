## What This Rules Out

This result rules out Eureka Pipe Line Company as a grantee in Lewis County deeds and oil-and-gas leases indexed in this General Index for 1817-1934. The E-company section was examined from Erbacon through Evans, and no Eureka Pipe Line Company spelling appears. The relevant section is absent, not obscured or damaged.

Important caveat: the Anna Copley to South Penn Oil Company lease at Book 42 page 267 would be indexed under South Penn in the grantee index, not under Eureka Pipe Line. If Eureka Pipe Line later acquired rights from South Penn by assignment, that assignment might have been separately recorded and indexed under Eureka Pipe Line. This index shows no such entry, so Eureka Pipe Line is a bounded negative as a Lewis County grantee through 1934.
