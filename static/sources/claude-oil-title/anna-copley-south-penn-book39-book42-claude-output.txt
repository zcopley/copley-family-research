FULL RESEARCH REPORT: Anna Copley / South Penn Oil Company, Lewis County, West Virginia

Critical finding: two separate leases

The grantee-index entry in Film 007902191 / DGS 7902191, image 310, references a lease in Deed Book 42, page 267. Deed Book 42 was not accessible online in the checked FamilySearch deed-book coverage. The Lewis County deed catalog entry checked online covered through Book 40, while Books 41-42 appear to require a separate courthouse / microfilm request.

A second related Anna Copley to South Penn Oil Company lease was located online in DGS 008293301, image 444, Book 39, page 138. That accessible lease involves the same family group and grantee, executed in the same year, and appears to be a companion or related lease for a different tract.

Lease term:

The report reads the lease as a standard South Penn form, apparently for two years from the date and as long thereafter as oil or gas is produced.

Oil royalty:

The report reads the oil royalty as one-eighth of oil produced and saved, delivered free of cost in the pipeline to which the wells may connect. The OCR was imperfect, but parallel South Penn forms support the one-eighth reading.

Gas royalty:

$24 per year for gas from each gas well whose product is marketed and used off the premises, with payment within sixty days after use begins and annually thereafter while so used.

Delay rental:

The lease would become null and void unless a well was completed within the stated period or delay rental paid quarterly in advance. Payments could be made directly to the lessors or deposited to their credit in the National [Exchange] Bank of Weston, West Virginia.

Assignment / surrender:

The report describes standard South Penn successor / assign language and a surrender clause allowing the second party to surrender the lease for cancellation, ending future payments and liabilities.

Witnesses / notary:

- Acknowledgment date: 18 July 1899

Recording certificate:

Clerk's Office, County Court, Lewis County, 6 February 1900. The oil and gas lease, legally stamped, with certificate annexed, was presented and admitted. E. A. Bennett, Clerk.

PART D: IDENTITY OF ANNA COPLEY

The report argues that Anna Copley is the widow Ann Copley, wife of the late Michael Copley, based on the co-signing daughters and the 1900 family exchange deed context. This is a strong inference for the accessible Book 39 lease because the co-lessors align with Michael and Ann's daughters. For the Book 42 page 267 Sand Fork lease, the report treats the Anna Copley identity as almost certainly the same widow, but the underlying Book 42 lease still needs to be read before that can be confirmed from the instrument itself.

PART E: DOES THE BOOK 39 LEASE NAME MICHAEL COPLEY HEIRS?

The Book 39 page 138 lease does not use the phrase "Michael Copley heirs." It names individual lessors: Anna Copley, Bridget Copley, Margrett Copley, and Sarah E. Copley.

The Book 42 page 267 lease has not yet been retrieved. Its index names only Anna Copley as grantor.
