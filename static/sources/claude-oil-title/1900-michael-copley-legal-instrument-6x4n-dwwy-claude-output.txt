MAIN RESULT

FamilySearch record https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6X4N-DWWY is a family land-exchange deed executed 22 August 1900 and recorded 13 October 1900. It is not the South Penn Oil Company lease, an administrator's deed, or a probate record.

Ann Copley and four daughters - Margaret Copley, Ann Copley, Sarah Copley, and Bridget Gillooly - together with Bridget's husband Patrick Gillooly, conveyed their interest in a Lewis County tract to John Copley. The deed recites an exchange involving a 55-acre tract associated with Michael Copley's will and 27.5 acres of John's interest in a 200-acre Sand Fork tract.

PROPERTY AND TITLE DETAILS

The deed recites:

- a 55-acre tract situated on Bungport Run of Cove Lick and associated with Michael Copley's will;
- 27.5 acres of John Copley's one-half interest in a 200-acre Sand Fork tract;
- an earlier deed dated 1 May 1878, with the book designation read in the image review as "Deed Book No. 1," page 321;
- a metes-and-bounds description containing 79 acres and 48 poles, more or less.

The exact volume number within the v. 38-39 image group, the older deed-book designation, and the relationship between the recited 55 acres and the final 79-acre/48-pole description should be confirmed from the complete deed image or a clerk copy.

OIL-LEASE RELEVANCE

The deed contains no oil, gas, mineral-rights, royalty, lease, or South Penn language. It does show a family title rearrangement affecting Sand Fork and Cove Lick property immediately before South Penn completed Michael Copley Heirs well No. 1 on 13 September 1900. The reason for the exchange and its relationship to the separate 128-acre oil lease remain unproven.

KEY EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES

- The deed directly confirms Ann, Margaret, Ann, Sarah, Bridget Gillooly, Patrick Gillooly, and John Copley as parties to the family land exchange.
- It directly confirms Michael Copley was deceased and a source of title.
- It does not identify the signers of the South Penn lease.
- It does not state royalty terms or prove who received oil income.
- It adds no evidence for Sarah Dolan.
