Image i3811884-00323 - Research Report

Source: Virginia, U.S., Personal Property Tax Records, 1782-1900; Ancestry collection 62774; image 324 of 646; tiles accessed at level 13 native / maximum zoom.

1. Page Type

Taxable-persons list page, same pre-printed form type as 00321. The form is a columnar personal-property tax return with data columns for tithables, horses, cattle, carriages, clocks, money / securities, joint-stock capital, aggregate value of personal property, and related categories.

2. Header Transcription

Printed: "List of Taxable Persons, Property, &c. within the District of"

Handwritten fill-in: "John" plus a second word. At maximum native zoom, the second word reads as a large looping capital letter followed by "s." and then "Ree." The character sequence is most consistently read as "Cs. Ree", meaning the abbreviation "Cs." followed by "Ree."

Full header: "List of Taxable Persons, Property, &c. within the District of John Cs. Ree"

Caveat: the large capital before "s. Ree" has a flourish that cannot be resolved beyond reasonable doubt at any zoom level. It could be read as a decorative "R" or a single extended "Ree" with flourish, but "Cs. Ree" is the most consistent read given the known context from 00321 and 00322.

3. Year Visible

No year is printed or written anywhere on the face of this page in a legible form. The title line has no year, no year appears in the column headers, and no handwritten year annotation appears in any margin.

4. District / Commissioner Label

The district label is "John Cs. Ree", the same commissioner / district name as on 00321. No separate commissioner block is visible; the district name in the header is the only identifier.

5. Whether It Continues the Ree District or Begins a New One

Directly continues the Ree district. The header is identical in form and district name to 00321. This is another data page for the same district.

6. Signatures, Oaths, Affidavits, Margin Notes, Written Commissioner Name

None found anywhere on the page. Margins, bottom tiles, and right-side tiles were checked. The left margin contains month labels and ditto marks only. No oath, affidavit, attestation, certification, or signature block appears on this page.

7. Month Labels

Upper block: May, approximately rows 1-11.

Lower block: April, approximately rows 18-29.

8. Taxable-Persons Name Range

May block, M range: Minter John B.; McLaughlin William; McCord Sarah; McKinley Edwin; McKinley Uriah; Magwell or Maxwell Levi; McGee James; Magwell or Maxwell R.; McGetiglaw or similar Michael; Miller Henry; Miller James W.

April block, N range: Norris Caroline or similar; Nicholas John; Nicholas Isaac; Nat [?] George W.; Nat [?] Rodlan / Rolan; Nat [?] Davis [?]; Norman Stephen; Norman Nelson; Norman William.

Surname range: M / Mc / Ma / Mi into N / Ni / No. This is an alphabetical continuation from the prior Ree pages.

9. Effect on the Ree / Loghan 1854 Interpretation

Neutral. The page is another taxable-persons data sheet in the same Ree district, carrying M and N surnames. It contains no year, no oath, no signature, and no content confirming or contradicting the 1854 date already established by 00320 and 00322. It does not introduce a new district and does not contain the Loghan names.
