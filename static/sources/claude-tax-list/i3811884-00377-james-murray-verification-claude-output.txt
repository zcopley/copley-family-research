Conclusion:

James Murray and Patrick Murry are unambiguously in the same district list sequence. Image 00377 (page 15) -> image 00378 (summary / proof page) -> image 00379 (page 16) is a continuous run. The summary page falls between their pages in the standard format. Both entries are under the same commissioner, same district, and almost certainly the same year, with final year digit cut off on the summary image.

Year:

- Summary page i3811884-00378 shows "for the year 185", with final digit cut off.
- Patrick Murry's record on i3811884-00379 is assigned probable 1854 based on the same cutoff and sequence.
- James Murray, being on the immediately preceding page of the same commissioner list, is from the same probable 1854 year.
- No visible final digit can be confirmed from the available scan.
