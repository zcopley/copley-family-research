INTERPRETATION

Is this likely the adult Patrick Murry later seen in 1860/1870/1871?
Yes, very likely. A single "1" in the white-male-tithable column (males >=21, not infirm) confirms he was a free adult white male aged 21 or older in the tax year. With only 1 horse valued at $50 and no recorded cattle, hogs, or pleasure carriages, his taxable estate was modest - consistent with a recent immigrant or young household head rather than an established farmer. That profile fits a mid-career Patrick Murry who would appear in census records in 1860/1870 with a growing household.

Does this confirm Patrick was taxable in Lewis County in 1854?
Yes, as far as this image can tell. He appears as line 14 on page 16 of a Lewis County personal property tax list filed within the 1851-1856 range. The task-reported year of 1854 is internally consistent with the "185_" date visible on the proof page and with the positioning of this image within the film sequence. He is recorded as owing tax (the "15 / 65" and "8" in the totals columns represent his liability), and he held at least one horse, making him a bona fide Lewis County resident subject to personal property taxation.
