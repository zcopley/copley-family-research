Lewis County, West Virginia - Settlement Book 34 page 176 and Probate Record P 4/155
Access Path Report

Bottom line:

Neither Settlement Book 34 page 176 nor Probate Record P 4/155 was found online or microfilmed in any repository accessible through FamilySearch, WVRHC ArchivesSpace, ArchiveGrid, or the WV State Archives digital catalog.

The 1970 Genealogical Society of Utah filming of Lewis County probate records captured wills, inventories / appraisements, and two specialty volumes, but did not film Settlement Books or Probate Record P. These are originals-only courthouse records, with a possible reading-room / checklist route through WVRHC A&M 0062.

Result:

No FamilySearch catalog entry exists for Lewis County Settlement Books, Commissioner of Accounts books, Fiduciary Settlements, or Probate Record P. Settlement Books 1-34+ were not filmed.

WVRHC A&M 0062:

Lewis County Archives A&M 0062 is described as court cases, 1775-1933, county record volumes, and private account books of Lewis County, 1817-1932. A bound-volumes checklist is available, but not digitized.

ArchivesSpace search within A&M 0062 returned no hits for settlement, settlement book, fiduciary, or probate record P. Subjects listed include Account books, Court records, Drugs and druggists, General stores, Mills, and Printers; no probate / fiduciary subject tags.

Interpretation:

The bound-volumes checklist should be consulted to confirm whether Settlement Books and Probate Record P transferred to WVRHC or remained at the courthouse. Given subject tagging and lack of probate terms, the fiduciary settlement volumes probably remain at the Lewis County Courthouse.

WV State Archives:

The WV State Archives may hold some Lewis County microfilm from earlier programs, but the county microfilm index page was unavailable / returned 403 or 404. Research requests must be submitted in writing with fee. The Archives appears lower probability for County Clerk fiduciary / Commissioner of Accounts books unless part of a bulk transfer.
