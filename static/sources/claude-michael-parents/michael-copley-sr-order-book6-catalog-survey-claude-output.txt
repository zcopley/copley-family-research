However, the Circuit Court handled civil and equity litigation — not will probate. Wills in WV were probated in the County Court. So "Order Book No. 6" in the will probate certificate almost certainly refers to the County Court Order Books v. 6, not the Circuit Court's separately-numbered Law Order Books. The Circuit Court's "v. 19" or "v. 20" would not be "Order Book No. 6."

Analysis of the Core Problem

The County Court Order Books v. 1–6 (1881–1901) are the only "Order Book No. 6" that exists in Lewis County for the relevant period, and it is already confirmed as Film 825098 / DGS 8614118.

The issue reported — that image 285 / page 496 shows Conrad/Woofter entries instead of Copley — points to one of two explanations:

Explanation A (most likely): Page 496 ≠ Image 285. Order Book v. 6 runs from 1897 to 1901. The book could span several hundred pages. Page 496 in book-page numbering may correspond to a different film image number than 285. Image 285 of the DGS group would be the 285th scanned frame from the start of the entire DGS group — which may include a title page, index, and prior material, meaning page 496 in the book could actually be film image ~285–295 but at a different alignment than expected, or significantly later if the book started well before page 1 of the image group.

Explanation B (less likely): Page-number error in the probate certificate. A scrivener error could have placed "496" when the actual page was different.

Recommended Next Actions

Priority 1: Browse images in DGS 8614118 (County Court Order Book v.6, 1897-1901) systematically around images 280–310, looking specifically for March 4, 1897 date entries. Bottom Line

No separate probate, fiduciary, estate, or administration order-book series exists for Lewis County, WV on FamilySearch. The will certificate's "Order Book No. 6, page 496" refers to the County Court Order Books v. 6 (Film 825098 / DGS 8614118) — the only series that fits. The Copley entry was not found at image 285 because the image/page correspondence may be off. The fastest resolution is to check the Index to Wills & Fiduciaries (DGS 7618825) to get a confirmed page reference, then re-examine County Court Order Book v. 6 at the correct image range.
