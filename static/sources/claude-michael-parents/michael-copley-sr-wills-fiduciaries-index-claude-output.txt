Interpretation of the "Order Book No. 6, Page 496" Problem

The index does not cite "Order Book No. 6, page 496." Instead, the index records the Order Book reference as Order Book 1-A, page 45.

The Will Book C certificate says "Order Book No. 6, page 496." The General Index to Fiduciary Matters was compiled retrospectively and uses a different numbering or labeling scheme for the early Lewis County order books. The old fiduciary/order books may have been renumbered or relabeled between 1897 and the 1970 compilation.

The mismatch suggests that:

1. The volume filmed as DGS 8614118 and labeled v. 6 may not be the volume the 1897 court called Order Book No. 6.
2. The index's 1-A designation may refer to the same book differently, with p. 45 as the actual location of the March 4, 1897 Michael Copley order, while p. 496 is either a transcription error in Will Book C or refers to a later fiduciary accounting order.
3. The additional index line showing P and 4/155 may point to a separate later order, possibly a settlement or partition-type entry.

The appraisement figures 11/59 likely refer to Appraisement/Inventory Book 11, page 59, a separate series. They are not order-book numbers.
