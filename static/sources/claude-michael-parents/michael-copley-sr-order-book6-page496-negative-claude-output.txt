Page 496 contents:

Page 496 contains two entries, both from approximately June-July 1900. Neither mentions Michael Copley, John Copley, or the Copley estate.

Entry 1 - Notary Public commission, June 1900:

State of West Virginia, County of Lewis, in the Clerk's Office of the County Court, County of Lewis, in vacation of said court, [?] day of June, 1900. John H. Conrad appeared before the Clerk and exhibited his commission, issued in 1900 by G. N. Atkinson or similar, appointing him a Notary Public. Conrad gave bond and qualified. Attested by E. A. Bennett, Clerk.

Entry 2 - Administration bond, Woofter estate, c. June-July 1900:

State of West Virginia, County of Lewis, in the Clerk's Office of the County Court, in vacation, on Monday the 30th day of June or July. On motion of Susan Woofter, appointed administratrix of Marshall J. Woofter, deceased, with Charles Woofter as surety, bond was approved and she qualified by oath. Attested by E. A. Bennett, Clerk.

Interpretation:

The probate certificate's "County Court Order Book No. 6, page 496" citation does not point to Michael Copley's entry in this Board of Commissioners order-book volume. The most likely explanations are:

1. The probate certificate cites a different, parallel probate or will order-book series, also numbered No. 6.
2. The entry appears in the early March-April 1897 pages of this same volume, and "496" is a certificate or transcription error for another page.
3. A Copley estate order appears around 1900 in another page or series, and the page citation was recorded incorrectly.
