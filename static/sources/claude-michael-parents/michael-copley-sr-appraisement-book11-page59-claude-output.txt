Full transcript / close abstract:

Title: "Appraisement Bill of the Personal Property of Michael Copley dec'd."

Introductory formula:

"We the undersigned appraisers appointed by the County Court of Lewis Co. W.Va on the 4th day of March 1897 to appraise the Personal Property of Michael Copley Deceased and on this 13th day of March 1897 proceeded to value the Same and Jointly and appraised the Following described property to wit:"

Date clarification:

The county court appointed the appraisers on 4 March 1897. The actual appraisement was conducted on 13 March 1897.

Inventory items as read:

1. Two head of horses, $25 each, $50.00.
2. One horse, $10.00.
3. One cow, $25.00.
4. One cow, $20.00.
5. One cow, $15.00.
6. Three cows, $25 each, $75.00.
7. Four yearling cattle, $50.00.
8. Seven hogs, approximately $14.00-$16.00.
9. Nineteen sheep, $38.00.
10. Three stacks hay, $15.00.
11. Farming utensils, $25.00.
12. Household and kitchen furniture, $16.00.

Total as written:

The manuscript total at the bottom of the column reads approximately $305.22. An arithmetic check of the read items gives a higher sum, depending partly on the hogs value. Treat the manuscript total and any individual uncertain value as needing verification from a courthouse copy or clearer image.

Certification:

"Given under our hands as appraisers on the day & date above mentioned"

Appraisers:

1. Cyrus Rafferty, or possibly Hafferty.
2. George Leasure, Leasin, or Leazin.
3. Luther McInaney, or possibly McElnaney.

Executor named:

"John Copley Executor"

This is the main genealogical finding on the page. It places John Copley in a formal executor role for Michael Copley Sr.'s personal-property appraisement in March-April 1897. It is consistent with the located will naming son John as executor, but the appraisement itself does not state John's relationship.

Court filing / clerk notation:

State of West Virginia, Clerk Office County Court Lewis County, April circa 10, 1897.

"The foregoing Appraisement Bill was this day presented in said Office and admitted to record"

Attest: Bennett, Clerk. First initials and filing day partly obscured; the surname Bennett is visible.

Relationship language:

- No parentage language.
- No spouse named.
- No children named in the appraisement.
- "John Copley Executor" is the only relationship-adjacent legal role on the page.

Estate character:

The inventory is personal property only. It describes a working farm estate with livestock, hay, farming utensils, and household / kitchen furniture. No real estate, books, Bible, notes, debts, or promissory notes appear on the appraisement page.

- The Copley appraisement is contained on page 59 and does not continue to image 61.
