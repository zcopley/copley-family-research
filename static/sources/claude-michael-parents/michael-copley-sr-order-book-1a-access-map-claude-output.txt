Part 2 - Identifying Order Book 1-A

The most probable explanation is that Order Book 1-A is a separate county-court probate / fiduciary order book series that was maintained independently and numbered with letter suffixes (1-A, 1-B, etc.) to distinguish it from the general order book series.

This is consistent with West Virginia practice in some counties, which kept a Commissioner of Accounts Order Book or Probate Order Book separate from the general County Court Order Books.

Page 45 points away from County Court Volume 5 because Volume 5 covers 1894-1897 and page 45 would fall early in the volume, ca. 1894-1895, not March 1897. The low page number paired with the March 1897 date fits a newly started fiduciary / probate order book series.

The catalog entry that may contain it is "Court records, 1817-1875; court record index, 1817-1932" (koha:268417, West Virginia County Court, Lewis County). It is a large multi-reel compilation and may contain miscellaneous county court records including fiduciary order books not covered by the dedicated 1881-1901 order-book series. The film list could not be read before session expiry.
