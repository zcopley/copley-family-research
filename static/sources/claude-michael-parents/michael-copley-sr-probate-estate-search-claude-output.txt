Abstract / Transcription of Key Lines

Opening, top of right page, image 437:
"In the name of God, I Michael Copley of the County of Lewis and State of West Va, being of sound mind and memory, do make and publish this my last will and testament..."

Land bequest:
"...I bequeath to my son John Copley fifty five acres, being [already?] sold him above his name... one hundred and twenty-seven and a half acres... running parallel with the old lines... this 55 acres to be included in this one hundred and twenty-seven and a half acres..."

Daughters' portion:
"...my land from [that?] on down to face [the] four daughters first mentioned on this will. This tract to be equally divided amongst Annie, Bridget, Margaret and Sarah [Copley] [after their] mothers death. The four girls mentioned [to take] out of their portion the sum of one hundred and twenty-five dollars to be distributed as follows: Catherne [Hannon?] fifty dollars; to Thomas [Copley] fifty dollars; to [Mary?] [Quinn?] twenty-five dollars. Wife and four daughters Annie, Bridget, Margaret, and Sarah to inherit all my personal property after my death - these same four girls to [divide] it equally among them after [their] mothers death. After defraying my funeral and their Mother's funeral expenses, also they to put a decent stone on our grave. I do nominate and appoint my son John Copley to be the sole administrator of this my last will and testament..."

Closing:

Parentage Relevance

The will does not name Michael Copley's parents. However, it:
1. Confirms identity: "Michael Copley of the County of Lewis and State of West Virginia," probated March 1897, matches the known subject.
2. Confirms children: names John, Annie, Bridget, Margaret, Sarah, Thomas, and very likely Catherine Hannon and Mary Quinn.
3. Confirms wife survived him: references "mothers death" and "wife" as still living at time of writing in 1893, though wife is not named by first name in the readable text.
4. Confirms executor as John Copley.
5. Establishes probate book location: Order Book No. 6, page 496, Lewis County, WV.

The death-register parentage clue, father John Copley and mother Sarah Copley, is not corroborated or contradicted by this will. The will is a first-person document, and Irish-born testators rarely named parents in American wills. To test the John / Sarah parent clue, the next record should be immigration, naturalization, baptismal, land, estate settlement, heirship, or church evidence.

Reliability Assessment

Grade: A - primary, official record, directly imaged from courthouse microfilm.

- The will book is a certified copy of the original will, recorded in Lewis County's official will books and probated by the County Court on March 4, 1897.
- The FamilySearch images are digitized from Genealogical Society of Utah Microfilm 819929, a film of records at the Lewis County Courthouse, DGS 4715576.
- The index entry has been attached in FamilySearch Family Tree to "Michael Copley, 1813-1897, L4ZS-B6W," consistent with the known subject.
- Handwriting is legible in key sections; several names are high confidence; some cash-bequest recipients have partially legible surnames.
- Date of will, 1893, is four years pre-death and entirely normal for a man about age 80.
