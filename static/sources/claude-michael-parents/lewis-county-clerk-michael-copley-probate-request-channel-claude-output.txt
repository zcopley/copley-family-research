Fee notes:

WV Code section 59-1-10(d) sets statutory minimums for the clerk of the county commission:

- $1.50 for a copy of any writing or document, first two pages.
- $1.00 for each additional page beyond two pages.
- $1.00 to annex the clerk's seal / certification to any paper.

The Lewis County Clerk website does not publish a specific fee schedule for historical probate / fiduciary copies beyond state code. Actual per-page fees, search charges, handling, certification, and payment instructions should be confirmed with the clerk before copies are made.

Recommended request workflow:

1. Call 304-269-8215 to confirm records are accessible and ask for current copy / certification fee and payment instructions.
2. Follow up by email or mail with a written request referencing the call.
3. Send check or money order payable to Lewis County Clerk for the quoted amount, or ask whether the office invoices first.
