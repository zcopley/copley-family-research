Volunteer Record Request Tasks

This page is for relatives and volunteers who want to help verify the Copley family history by requesting records from courthouses, archives, churches, libraries, and cemeteries.

Before sending a request, please email Zach Copley at zach@copley.name or Tom Copley at tcopley@protonmail.com with the task ID you want to take. That prevents duplicate letters to the same office.

How To Help

  1. Pick one task below.
  2. Email Zach or Tom with the task ID, for example VR-01 Luke Murray bond.
  3. Verify the office address, email, web form, or fax number on the official website before sending.
  4. Send the request.
  5. Save a copy of what you sent.
  6. When you receive a response, forward the response, scans, invoice, or negative result to Zach and Tom.

Do not pay large fees without checking first. Small copy fees are normal, but anything above about $25 should be approved before payment.

How To Report Results

Please send results to:

  • Zach Copley: zach@copley.name
  • Tom Copley: tcopley@protonmail.com

Use this subject line format:

Please include:

If a record is found, please send the scan or photo plus any envelope, cover note, invoice, or citation page. If nothing is found, the negative response is still useful evidence.

VR-01: Luke Murray / Anne Loghan Marriage Bond

Claim tested: Whether the 1854 Lewis County marriage of Luke Murray and Anne Loghan has a surviving bond, consent paper, surety record, or bond-book entry.

Best recipient: Lewis County Circuit Clerk’s Office, Beth Burkhart, Circuit Clerk, Lewis County Courthouse Annex, 117 Court Avenue, Weston, WV 26452. Phone 304-269-8210. Fax 304-269-8249.

Secondary recipient if referred: Lewis County Clerk, 110 Center Avenue, Weston, WV 26452. Phone 304-269-8215.

VR-02: Michael Copley Sr. Probate Order and Estate Packet

Claim tested: Whether Michael Copley Sr. left estate records beyond the will that name heirs, sureties, appraisers, land divisions, Bible evidence, or parents.

Best recipient: Start with Lewis County Clerk, 110 Center Avenue, Weston, WV 26452. If referred, contact the Lewis County Circuit Clerk.

Known restricted film targets:

  • County Court Order Book v. 6, 1897-1901: Film 825098 / DGS 8614118; page 496 is now checked and negative for Copley, but early March 1897 pages remain a possible page-citation-error check.
  • County Court Order Book v. 5, 1894-1897: Film 825097 / DGS 8614117; check for January-March 1897 preliminary entries.
  • Index to wills & fiduciaries, 1817-1970: Film 819938 / DGS 7618825; next best index check to confirm the Copley order-book citation.
  • Wills, inventories, etc., Lewis County, 1853-1899 / Inventories, etc., Lewis County, 1853-1900: Film 250047 / DGS 7617564.

VR-02A: Michael Copley Restricted Probate-Film Lookup

Claim tested: Same as VR-02, but this task is for a volunteer who can use a FamilySearch Center, the FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City, or WVU’s West Virginia and Regional History Center remote reference / reproduction service.

Best recipient / method: FamilySearch Center or FamilySearch Library for restricted DGS access. For WVU WVRHC, use their current official remote reference / reproduction request path and verify current procedures before submitting.

VR-03: Michael and Patrick Copley Naturalization Records

Claim tested: Whether naturalization records identify Michael or Patrick Copley with more precise Irish birthplace, arrival, oath date, or court details.

Best recipients: Lewis County Clerk, Lewis County Circuit Clerk, and West Virginia State Archives. Verify which office holds county-court naturalization records before sending.

VR-04: St. Michael’s and Early Catholic Records

Claim tested: Whether early Catholic sacramental records identify Michael Copley, Ann Munday, their children’s baptisms, marriage sponsors, or Murray / Dolan / Hannon / Gillooly settlement links.

Best recipient: The parish or diocesan archive responsible for historic Catholic records for St. Michael’s / St. Bernard’s / Lewis County, WV. Verify current parish and Diocese of Wheeling-Charleston record-request instructions before sending.

VR-05: Ann Munday / Kinawley Tithe and Land Records

Claim tested: Whether unindexed Kinawley records support Ann’s recorded Munday / Monday / Mundy surname, or whether Murray appears only as a separate neighborhood clue.

Best recipient: PRONI or another repository holding Kinawley, County Fermanagh tithe, church, estate, or land records. Verify current PRONI enquiry instructions before sending.

VR-06: Copley No. 1 Oil Leases, Royalties, and John Copley Estate

Claim tested: Who signed and benefited from the recorded lease behind the now-verified Michael Copley Heirs 128-Acre Lease, and whether related lease, royalty, title-chain, mineral-rights, probate, or estate records document family finances.

Best recipient: Lewis County Clerk for deeds, leases, title-chain records, and probate material. The official county page lists: Lewis County Clerk’s Office, 110 Center Avenue, Weston, WV 26452; phone 304-269-8215; fax 304-269-8202; email lewiscountyclerk@lewiscountywv.org. The county’s general courthouse number is 304-269-8200.

Known evidence and index targets:

  • David B. Reger’s 1927 AAPG article identifies Michael Copley Heirs well No. 1, completed 13 September 1900, and the Michael Copley Heirs 128-Acre Lease, operated by South Penn.
  • The South Penn grantee index identifies Anna Copley to South Penn Oil Company, Lease, Book 42 page 267, 128-A Sand Fk ORD / Glenville & Court House, on Film 819926 / DGS 007902191, image 310. Online access-map work found no online deed-book image for Book 42; the checked FamilySearch deed-book run appears to end with v. 40.
  • A related online companion lease appears at Book 39 page 138, Film / DGS 008293301 image 444: Anna Copley, Bridget Copley, Margrett / Margaret Copley, and Sarah E. Copley to South Penn Oil Company, executed 18 July 1899 and recorded 6 February 1900 for a Land Fork tract.
  • FamilySearch record 6X4N-DWWY, DGS 008293301, image 245, pages 422-423, is a separate family exchange deed executed 22 August 1900 and recorded 13 October 1900. It involves Sand Fork and a 55-acre tract with unresolved place-name reading, but contains no oil-lease language. Its cited older deed is now visually verified at Deed Book 11 page 321, DGS 008293286 images 193-194: Harrison / Edwards / Sullivan as administrator of Emma M. Hoffman to Michael and John Copley, 200 acres on Land Fork / Sand Fork / Cove Lick, executed 1 May 1880 and recorded 24 February 1881.
  • Copley grantor index: Film 819917 / DGS 8219260. This path is currently negative: the relevant index sequence jumps from Colvin at image 305 to Cork at image 306 with no Copley page or entry.
  • Copley grantee index: Film 819924 / DGS 8135143.
  • South Penn grantee index: Film 819926 / DGS 7902191. Anna Copley match found at image 310; checked South Penn pages show no other Copley entry in the reported range. The same film’s Standard Oil path is now closed as a bounded negative: images 310-360 pass from South Penn to Smith to Stalnaker to Swisher, with no Standard Oil page or Copley-related Standard Oil entry. Eureka Pipe Line is also closed as a bounded negative in Film 819924 / DGS 8135143: the correct E-company section at image 437 contains only Eureka Lumber & Manufacturing Company entries and blank rows before Evans Supply Store, with no Eureka Pipe Line / Pipeline entry.

VR-06A: Restricted Lewis County Deed / Offline-Film Lookup

Claim tested: Whether any FamilySearch Library, FamilySearch Center, state-archive, or other offline path can reach Lewis County Deed Book 42, page 267 without a clerk request. The book/page target itself is already identified; the remaining problem is access to the unfilmed / offline deed-book text. The 21 March 1886 John Copley marginal-note deed-index path is now a bounded negative in the checked FamilySearch deed-book / deed-index materials.

Best recipient / method: A volunteer with access to a FamilySearch Center or the FamilySearch Library. Send results to Zach or Tom using the reporting format at the top of this page.

VR-06B: WVRHC Copley Heirs Well Archive Lookup

Claim tested: Whether WVU’s West Virginia and Regional History Center holds photographs, captions, correspondence, or company records that identify the Sand Fork lease, its owners, or related South Penn operations.

Best recipient: West Virginia and Regional History Center, WVU Libraries. The report supplied wvrhc@mail.wvu.edu; verify the current official contact and reproduction procedures before sending.

VR-07: Mary Copely Giblin and Iowa Copely Branch

Claim tested: Whether Mary Copely Giblin of Iowa was a sibling or close relative of Michael Copley Sr. and whether Iowa records name parents, siblings, or County Roscommon origins.

Best recipients: Crawford County, Iowa courthouse; local genealogical society; Mitchellville Cemetery contact; relevant Catholic parish or diocesan archive.

VR-08: Benjamin Partlow Revolutionary Pension and Proof Chain

Claim tested: Whether Benjamin Partlow can be proven as the Revolutionary ancestor in the Partlow line.

Best recipients: NARA for the full pension file; county clerks or archives in the relevant Virginia and Illinois counties for marriage, probate, land, and tax records.

VR-09: Staunton-Parkersburg Turnpike and Irish Labor Records

Claim tested: Whether Michael Copley, Patrick Copley, or the Lewis County Irish Catholic settlement network can be connected to turnpike, railroad, contractor, payroll, or infrastructure-labor records.

Best recipient: West Virginia State Archives or a local historical repository holding Staunton-Parkersburg Turnpike, Lewis County court, contractor, or road records.

Current Evidence Cautions

  • Do not report Sarah Dolan as proven. The 1897 death register names Michael Copley’s parents as John Copley and Sarah Copley, but no located record gives Sarah’s maiden surname as Dolan.
  • Do not report Ann Copley as Murray unless a direct record says so. Munday remains the recorded family surname form.
  • Do not treat online family trees as proof unless they cite an original or near-original record.
  • A negative result is still useful if the office says exactly what books, indexes, or years were checked.
